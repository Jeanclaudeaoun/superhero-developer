# Rewards & Affiliation

> **Page brief** — Document every way to earn on Superhero without trading, and be precise about eligibility caveats.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

There are two distinct programs, and they are often confused:

- **The Superhero Rewards Program** — milestone-based AE rewards for verifying your X
  account, inviting users and posting about Superhero.
- **The Trading Affiliate Program** — a four-level referral system paid out of the 0.5%
  affiliation share of every token buy.
