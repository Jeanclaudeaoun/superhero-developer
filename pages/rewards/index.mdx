# Rewards & Affiliation

Superhero pays out AE through two completely separate systems. They share a page in the app
(`/trends/invite`) and they are routinely confused, so start here before reading anything
else in this section.

## Two programs, one page

| | Superhero Rewards Program | Trading Affiliate Program |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you do | Link your X account, post about Superhero | Create funded invite links, get people trading |
| Who pays | Superhero's backend, off-chain scan of your X posts | The bonding curve — 0.5% of every buy in your downline |
| Where the money comes from | A discretionary program budget | Protocol fees, split on-chain |
| Payout | Sent automatically to your connected wallet | You call `withdraw()` on the AffiliationTreasury contract |
| Gate | X account with at least 100 followers | 4 direct invitees who have each spent at least 10 AE |
| Ceiling | The FAQ quotes "up to 200 AE", but no cap exists in the backend — one rewarded post per UTC day is the real limit | Uncapped per period, but see the deadline below |
| Deadline | None | **Rewards expire.** Only the current 30-day payout period and the previous one are withdrawable |
| Can be switched off | Yes, at any time without notice | No, it is contract logic |

The **Superhero Rewards Program** is a growth campaign. It is off-chain, discretionary,
geo-restricted, and explicitly not guaranteed.

The **Trading Affiliate Program** is protocol mechanics. Every community-token buy carries a
1% spread; half of it goes to the community DAO and half to affiliation. That affiliation
half is split across up to four levels of referrers above the buyer, and any level with no
referrer is credited to nobody — the AE stays in the treasury contract permanently, which is
what the whitepaper means by "burned".

Two things about it surprise people, both settled from
`bcl-contracts/contracts/AffiliationTreasury.aes`:

- **A partial chain strands the rest, not just an unaffiliated buy.** A buyer two levels deep
  pays the full 0.5% but only 0.425% of it reaches anyone.
- **Accrued rewards expire after 30 to 60 days.** `withdraw()` reaches the current 30-day
  payout period and the one before it, and nothing else. Withdraw monthly.

## The affiliation split at a glance

```text
Buyer pays AE for a community token
  ├── 99.0%  → bonding curve reserve (redeemable by sellers)
  ├──  0.5%  → community DAO treasury
  └──  0.5%  → affiliation system
                ├── 0.300% → level 1 (direct inviter)
                ├── 0.125% → level 2
                ├── 0.050% → level 3
                └── 0.025% → level 4
                (any level with no recipient: stranded in the treasury forever)
```

The 0.5% is added on top of the curve price rather than carved out of it, so a buy whose
curve price is 1,000 AE costs the buyer 1,005 AE. Source:
`bcl-contracts/contracts/AffiliationTreasury.aes` and
`bcl-contracts/contracts/AffiliationBondingCurveTokenSale.aes`, which is where these figures
are `let`-bound constants — the whitepaper's "Affiliation Bonding Curve Token Sale" and
"Token Economics Summary" sections describe the same split in prose.
