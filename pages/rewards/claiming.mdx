# Claiming Rewards

Three different things on Superhero are called "claiming a reward", and they behave
completely differently. One is automatic, one is a contract call you sign, and one is signed
by a throwaway key embedded in an invite link. This page separates them and lists the errors
you will actually see.

## The three payout paths

| Reward | How it reaches you | Who signs | Gas |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X posting rewards | Sent automatically to your connected wallet after a successful scan | Nobody — the backend pays out | None for you |
| Affiliate rewards | You press **Collect rewards**, which calls `withdraw()` on the AffiliationTreasury | You | Yes, an ordinary æternity transaction fee |
| An invite link's AE gift | You open the link and press **Claim reward** | The invite's one-time key, not your wallet | Covered by the `redemption_fee_cover` the inviter funded |

## X posting rewards — nothing to claim

There is no claim button for the [Superhero Rewards Program](/rewards/rewards-program).
Press **Check rewards** on `/trends/invite`, sign the ownership challenge with your wallet,
and the backend scans and pays. The earnings banner confirms it: rewards "have been sent
automatically to your connected wallet".

What can go wrong:

- **The button is greyed out with a countdown.** The recheck is rate-limited. The status
  response carries `next_check_allowed_at`; a submit past the cap returns HTTP 429 and the
  scan is skipped entirely. Wait for the countdown and try again.
- **The check succeeds but nothing arrives.** A recheck that returns HTTP 200 can still
  carry an `error` string — below the follower minimum, the X identity was already rewarded
  on a different address, or the payout transaction itself failed. The card shows that
  string in a red banner. It is the real reason; read it rather than retrying blindly.
- **You rejected the signature.** The client swallows wallet rejections silently and leaves
  the state unchanged. Press the button again and approve the signature request.

## Affiliate rewards — the withdraw call

Affiliate rewards accrue in the `AffiliationTreasury` contract as your invitees trade. They
sit there until you withdraw them.

**The gate:** four direct invitees must each have spent at least 10 AE, cumulatively, in
token sales. The Collect Rewards card reads this live from the contract, shows a progress bar
out of four, and lists each invitee's spend against the 10 AE threshold.

To withdraw:

1. Open `https://superhero.com/trends/invite` and connect the wallet that created the
   invites. Rewards accrue to the inviter address, not to any other account.
2. Scroll to **Collect your rewards**. The card shows **Available Rewards** — the value of
   `get_accumulated_rewards(<your address>)`, converted from aettos.
3. If the progress bar reads 4/4 and the balance is above zero, the button is enabled. Press
   **Collect rewards** and confirm the transaction in your wallet. It calls `withdraw()`.

Button states and what they mean:

| Button reads | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not eligible yet | Fewer than four invitees have crossed 10 AE |
| No rewards yet | Threshold reached, but `get_accumulated_rewards` returns zero |
| Collect rewards | Eligible; the call will go through |
| Withdrawing... | Transaction submitted, waiting on the wallet and the chain |

Contract errors the card translates for you:

- `MINIMUM_ACCOUNTS_THRESHOLD_NOT_REACHED` and `MINIMUM_OF_4_ACCOUNTS_SHALL_BUY` both render
  as "you need 4 direct invitees who each spent at least 10 AE in token sales". If you see
  this while the bar shows 4/4, the on-chain state disagrees with the cached UI — reload.
- A `Key does not exist` or `Maps:` error from `get_unique_invitee` means the contract has no
  entry for your address yet. The card treats that as zero progress, not as a failure. It is
  the normal state for an account that has never invited anyone.

## An invite link's AE gift — claimed by the link, not by you

When someone opens an invite link, the app reads `invite_code` out of the URL fragment,
reconstructs a one-time account from the secret it contains, and shows a claim card with the
funded amount and who sent it.

Pressing **Claim reward** calls `redeem_invitation_code(<your wallet address>)` **signed by
the invite's one-time key**, not by your wallet. That is why the inviter funds a
`redemption_fee_cover` alongside the gift: the throwaway account needs enough AE to pay for
its own redemption transaction. Your wallet only has to be connected so the contract knows
where to send the AE and whom to attach to the affiliation tree.

Errors you may hit:

| Error | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| `ALREADY_REDEEMED` | The link was already claimed. The card also detects this up front by reading the third field of `get_invitation_code`. |
| `INVITEE_ALREADY_REGISTERED` | Your wallet address is already attached to an inviter. One account can only be invited once. |
| `Trying to call undefined function` | You are on the wrong network — the treasury at that address does not exist on the network your wallet is set to. |
| A message mentioning `Secret key` | The secret in the link is in a format the SDK cannot load. The link is malformed or truncated. |

If you are the **inviter** and you open your own link, the card switches to **Revoke
invitation** instead of Claim, and calls `revoke_invitation_code` with your own wallet.

## Open questions

- **Does the FAQ's "claim banner" refer to something else?** The FAQ describes a claim banner
  appearing on the Rewards page once a milestone completes, with a claim button and a gas
  fee. The current rewards UI pays X posting rewards automatically with no such banner; the
  only claim banner in the app is the invite-link card described above. Either the FAQ
  predates the automatic payout, or a milestone claim flow exists that is not in this build.
  Settling it needs the deployed backend, not the web app.
- **Do unclaimed rewards expire?** Nothing in the client sets a deadline on accumulated
  affiliate rewards or on an unclaimed invite link. The contract's payout-period accounting
  (see [The Affiliation System](/rewards/affiliation)) is the place to check, and the ACI
  alone does not answer it.
- **What happens to the AE in a revoked link?** The CLI help calls revoke "reclaim funds",
  which implies the gift returns to the inviter, but neither the ACI nor the client confirms
  whether the `redemption_fee_cover` portion comes back with it.
