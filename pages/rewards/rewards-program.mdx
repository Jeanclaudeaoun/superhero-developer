# Superhero Rewards Program

The Superhero Rewards Program pays AE for promoting Superhero on X. You link your X account
to your on-chain SuperheroID, post about Superhero, and the backend scans your posts and sends
AE to your connected wallet.

This is **not** the affiliate program. Affiliate rewards come from trading fees and are
withdrawn from a contract — see [The Affiliation System](/rewards/affiliation).

## Where it lives

The program is at the top of `https://superhero.com/trends/invite`, above the Refer & Earn
cards. Connect your wallet first; nothing on the page tracks progress without an active
account.

## Milestone 1 — Link X Account & Post

**Reward: 50 AE.** Two steps, both of which must complete before milestone 2 unlocks.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Link your X account to your on-chain SuperheroID. The button sends you to your profile page at `/users/<your address>`. |
| 2 | Post about Superhero from that linked account. The post must mention Superhero — either a `superhero.com` link or a tag of `@superhero_chain`. |

The app pre-fills the compose intent with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! https://superhero.com
```

- **Your X account must have at least 100 followers.** Accounts under that threshold are not
  eligible.
- Milestone 1 is a **regular post** — it does not need your referral link. Only milestone 2
  requires the link.

Linking X also sets up your SuperheroID profile. See [SuperheroID](/identity/superhero-id)
and [X verification](/identity/x-verification).

## Milestone 2 — Post on X & Earn

**Reward: 10–30 AE per qualifying post, plus a 50 AE streak bonus.** Locked until milestone 1
is paid.

Every post must contain **your unique referral link** in the post text. The link is minted
for you the first time you press "Post on X" on the rewards card; the app fetches it, then
opens an X compose intent pre-filled with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! <your referral link>
```

Your per-post rate is set by your follower count:

| Follower count | Per qualifying post |
| --- | --- |
| Under 100 | Not eligible |
| 100 – 10,000 | 10 AE |
| 10,000 – 1,000,000 | 20 AE |
| Over 1,000,000 | 30 AE |

Two counters run on this milestone:

- **Posts rewarded** — the card tracks progress toward **10 rewarded posts** and reads
  "completed" at 10.
- **Streak** — posting on 10 consecutive days earns an **extra 50 AE**. The card shows your
  current streak out of 10 days. The bonus is recurring: when the streak reaches 10 it pays
  out and the counter resets, so the next streak starts from zero.

At the base tier, milestone 1 plus 10 posts plus one streak bonus adds up to 200 AE:
50 AE + 10 × 10 AE + 50 AE. Earnings continue past that at one rewarded post per UTC day.

## The three payout paths

| Path | Trigger | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Your first post containing `superhero.com` or `superhero_chain` | One-time |
| Per-post | A post containing your referral link | Once per UTC day, at your follower tier's rate |
| Streak bonus | 10 consecutive UTC days of referral posts | Recurring — the counter resets and starts again |

An account below the minimum follower count accrues nothing — no qualifying posts, no
per-post rewards, no streak progress — though already-accrued payouts still settle.

### What counts as a qualifying post

- **Referral posts are matched on the host.** A URL in the post must have host `superhero.com`
  and a `?ref=` query parameter equal to your referral code, case-insensitively. X's truncated
  `display_url` values are accepted only when they start with `superhero.com` followed by `/`,
  `?`, `#` or the end of the string, so `evil.com/?u=superhero.com&ref=<code>` and
  `superhero.com.evil.com/?ref=<code>` do not qualify.
- **One rewarded post per UTC day.** A second referral post on the same UTC day earns nothing.
  A tweet whose `created_at` cannot be parsed into a UTC day earns nothing either.
- **A streak day is a UTC day.** The streak increments only when a qualifying day is exactly
  one after the last qualifying day. Any larger gap resets the streak to 1. The streak is
  anchored to UTC midnight, not to your local time or to 24-hour spacing.
- **Old posts fall off the back.** A scan covers the 2,000 newest tweets and advances a
  forward-only cursor even when it truncates, so posts older than that are never counted. The
  status response reports this as `x_posts_scan_truncated`.
- **Nothing is clawed back.** Payouts are on-chain AE spends and the ledger is insert-only.
  Deleting a tweet after it has been rewarded stops it appearing in future scans but does not
  reclaim the AE.

## How verification works

Nothing here is on-chain until the payout. The flow is a signed request to the Superhero
backend, which scans X on your behalf.

1. The rewards card calls `GET /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward` on load and renders
   your status, rewarded post count, streak, follower count, tier and referral link.
2. Pressing **Check rewards** requests a challenge from
   `POST /api/profile/x-posting-reward/recheck-challenge`. The response carries a `nonce`,
   an expiry and a `message`.
3. Your wallet signs that message. The signature, nonce and expiry are submitted to
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward/recheck`, which runs the scan and returns
   your updated status.
4. Minting the referral link uses the same signed proof against
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-reward/referral-link`.

**The recheck is rate-limited to one scan per 24 hours.** The status response carries
`next_check_allowed_at`; until that timestamp passes the button is disabled and shows a
countdown. A submit past the cap returns HTTP 429 and the scan is skipped. The cap is claimed
atomically, so retries and concurrent requests cannot exceed it.

- A handled scan failure — an X outage, say — still consumes the slot. An unexpected failure
  such as a database error releases it.
- Recovering a pending or failed payout never calls X, so it runs **before** the cap check. A
  recheck that finds a stuck payout can settle it even while the scan itself is cooling down.

**A successful check can still pay nothing.** A recheck that returns HTTP 200 may carry an
`error` string explaining why no reward was sent — below the follower minimum, the X identity
was already rewarded on another address, or the payout transaction failed. The card shows that
string as a red banner.

**Payouts are automatic.** AE is sent to your connected wallet by the backend; there is no
claim transaction and no gas cost to you. See [Claiming Rewards](/rewards/claiming) for the
rewards that *do* need a transaction.

## Eligibility

The program is discretionary: rewards are not guaranteed, the program can be paused at any
time, and users from restricted regions are not eligible. The full terms are shown at the foot
of the rewards page. Rewards may be reportable as income.
