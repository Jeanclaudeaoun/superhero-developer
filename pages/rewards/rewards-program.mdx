# Superhero Rewards Program

The Superhero Rewards Program pays AE for promoting Superhero on X. You link your X account
to your on-chain SuperheroID, post about Superhero, and the backend scans your posts and
sends AE to your connected wallet. This page documents both milestones, the follower tiers
that set your per-post rate, how verification actually runs, and the eligibility rules that
limit the whole thing.

This is **not** the affiliate program. Affiliate rewards come from trading fees and are
withdrawn from a contract — see [The Affiliation System](/rewards/affiliation).

## Where it lives

The program is rendered at the top of `https://superhero.com/trends/invite`, above the
Refer & Earn cards. Connect your wallet first; nothing on the page tracks progress without
an active account.

## Milestone 1 — Link X Account & Post

**Reward: 50 AE.** Two steps, both of which must complete before milestone 2 unlocks.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Link your X account to your on-chain SuperheroID. The button sends you to your profile page at `/users/<your address>`. |
| 2 | Post about Superhero from that linked account. The post must mention Superhero — either a `superhero.com` link or a tag of `@superhero_chain`. |

The app pre-fills the compose intent with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! https://superhero.com
```

Two conditions apply that the FAQ does not mention:

- **Your X account must have at least 100 followers.** Accounts under that threshold are not
  eligible for this reward.
- Milestone 1 is a **regular post** — it does not need your referral link. Only milestone 2
  requires the link.

Linking X also sets up your SuperheroID profile automatically. SuperheroID is the identity
contract that ties your social accounts to your wallet address; see
[SuperheroID](/identity/superhero-id) and [X verification](/identity/x-verification).

## Milestone 2 — Post on X & Earn

**Reward: 10–30 AE per qualifying post, plus a 50 AE streak bonus.** Locked until milestone 1
is paid.

Every post must contain **your unique referral link** in the post text. The link is minted
for you the first time you press "Post on X" on the rewards card; the app fetches it, then
opens an X compose intent pre-filled with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! <your referral link>
```

Your per-post rate is set by your follower count:

| Follower count | Per qualifying post |
| --- | --- |
| Under 100 | Not eligible |
| 100 – 10,000 | 10 AE |
| 10,000 – 1,000,000 | 20 AE |
| Over 1,000,000 | 30 AE |

Two counters run on this milestone:

- **Posts rewarded** — the card tracks progress toward **10 rewarded posts**. The milestone
  reads "completed" at 10.
- **Streak** — posting on 10 consecutive days earns an **extra 50 AE**. The card shows your
  current streak out of 10 days.

At the base tier those numbers add up to exactly the figure the FAQ quotes:
50 AE (milestone 1) + 10 posts × 10 AE + 50 AE (streak) = 200 AE.

## How verification works

Nothing here is on-chain until the payout. The flow is a signed request to the Superhero
backend, which scans X on your behalf.

1. The rewards card calls `GET /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward` on load and renders
   your status, rewarded post count, streak, follower count, tier and referral link.
2. Pressing **Check rewards** first requests a challenge from
   `POST /api/profile/x-posting-reward/recheck-challenge`. The response carries a `nonce`,
   an expiry and a `message`.
3. Your wallet signs that message. The signature, nonce and expiry are submitted to
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward/recheck`, which runs the scan and returns
   your updated status.
4. Minting the referral link uses the same signed proof against
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-reward/referral-link`.

**The recheck is rate-limited.** The status response carries `next_check_allowed_at`; until
that timestamp passes the button is disabled and shows a countdown. A submit past the cap
returns HTTP 429 and the scan is skipped. The client code documents this as a 24-hour cap.

**A successful check can still pay nothing.** A recheck that returns HTTP 200 may carry an
`error` string explaining why no reward was sent — below the follower minimum, the X identity
was already rewarded on another address, or the payout transaction failed. The card surfaces
that string as a red banner rather than silently reporting "no change".

**Payouts are automatic.** The earnings banner states plainly: "Your AE rewards have been
sent automatically to your connected wallet." There is no claim transaction for this program
and therefore no gas cost to you. See [Claiming Rewards](/rewards/claiming) for the rewards
that *do* need a transaction.

## Eligibility — read this before participating

This is the disclaimer shown at the foot of the rewards page, reproduced in substance:

> The Superhero reward program rewards users for creating content on other social media
> platforms (like X) with backlinks and/or invite links, referring new users to the platform.
> Rewards will be distributed in the form of AE tokens. **Eligibility and rewards depend on
> on-chain activity, are not guaranteed and can be paused at any time without notice or
> liability.** The right is reserved to disqualify any user from the program. **Users from
> blacklisted countries are not eligible for rewards.** By participating in the program,
> users agree to these terms and conditions. Rewards sent may be subject to tax reporting.
> Users are responsible for any tax obligations arising from receiving rewards.

Concretely, that means:

- **Nothing is owed to you.** Completing a milestone does not create a claim.
- **The program can stop mid-stream.** It can be paused at any time, without notice, with no
  liability for work already done.
- **Geography matters.** Users in blacklisted countries are not eligible. The list is not
  published in the app.
- **Individual disqualification is allowed** at the operator's discretion.
- **Rewards are income.** They may be reportable, and the tax consequences are yours.

## Open questions

- **Is 200 AE a hard cap?** The FAQ says "earn up to 200 AE tokens", and the base tier
  arithmetic lands exactly on 200. But the UI caps the post counter at 10 while paying
  20 AE or 30 AE per post at higher follower tiers, which would exceed 200 AE. Nothing in
  the client enforces a 200 AE ceiling. Settling this needs the backend reward service, not
  the web app.
- **Which countries are blacklisted?** The list is enforced server-side and is not present
  in the client bundle or the FAQ.
- **What counts as a "qualifying post"?** The client only reads
  `per_post_total_paid_count` and `current_streak_days` back from the API. The matching
  rules — how the referral link is detected, whether deleted posts are clawed back, how a
  streak day is bounded — live in the backend scanner.
- **Is the 24-hour recheck cap exact?** The client comment says 24 hours, but the actual
  window is whatever `next_check_allowed_at` says, which the server sets.
