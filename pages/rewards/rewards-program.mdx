# Superhero Rewards Program

> **Page brief** — Document the program, its phases and its caveats. The eligibility disclaimer is not optional — include it.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

The program lets you earn AE by completing milestones: verifying your X account, inviting new
users, and posting about Superhero on social media. Completing milestones unlocks phases,
with up to **200 AE** available.

| Milestone | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Verify X account & post | Connect your X account and post to prove ownership |
| Link X to SuperheroID | Record the link on-chain |
| Post about Superhero | Publish 10 posts that include your referral link in the text |

**Eligibility.** Rewards depend on on-chain activity and are **not guaranteed**. The program
can be paused at any time without notice. Users from blacklisted countries are not eligible.
Participating means agreeing to the terms shown on the rewards page.
