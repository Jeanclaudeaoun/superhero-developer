# Superhero Rewards Program

The Superhero Rewards Program pays AE for promoting Superhero on X. You link your X account
to your on-chain SuperheroID, post about Superhero, and the backend scans your posts and
sends AE to your connected wallet. This page documents both milestones, the follower tiers
that set your per-post rate, how verification actually runs, and the eligibility rules that
limit the whole thing.

This is **not** the affiliate program. Affiliate rewards come from trading fees and are
withdrawn from a contract — see [The Affiliation System](/rewards/affiliation).

## Where it lives

The program is rendered at the top of `https://superhero.com/trends/invite`, above the
Refer & Earn cards. Connect your wallet first; nothing on the page tracks progress without
an active account.

## Milestone 1 — Link X Account & Post

**Reward: 50 AE.** Two steps, both of which must complete before milestone 2 unlocks.

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Link your X account to your on-chain SuperheroID. The button sends you to your profile page at `/users/<your address>`. |
| 2 | Post about Superhero from that linked account. The post must mention Superhero — either a `superhero.com` link or a tag of `@superhero_chain`. |

The app pre-fills the compose intent with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! https://superhero.com
```

Two conditions apply that the FAQ does not mention:

- **Your X account must have at least 100 followers.** Accounts under that threshold are not
  eligible for this reward.
- Milestone 1 is a **regular post** — it does not need your referral link. Only milestone 2
  requires the link.

Linking X also sets up your SuperheroID profile automatically. SuperheroID is the identity
contract that ties your social accounts to your wallet address; see
[SuperheroID](/identity/superhero-id) and [X verification](/identity/x-verification).

## Milestone 2 — Post on X & Earn

**Reward: 10–30 AE per qualifying post, plus a 50 AE streak bonus.** Locked until milestone 1
is paid.

Every post must contain **your unique referral link** in the post text. The link is minted
for you the first time you press "Post on X" on the rewards card; the app fetches it, then
opens an X compose intent pre-filled with:

```text
Check out @superhero_chain — earn AE tokens by posting on X! <your referral link>
```

Your per-post rate is set by your follower count:

| Follower count | Per qualifying post |
| --- | --- |
| Under 100 | Not eligible |
| 100 – 10,000 | 10 AE |
| 10,000 – 1,000,000 | 20 AE |
| Over 1,000,000 | 30 AE |

Two counters run on this milestone:

- **Posts rewarded** — the card tracks progress toward **10 rewarded posts**. The milestone
  reads "completed" at 10.
- **Streak** — posting on 10 consecutive days earns an **extra 50 AE**. The card shows your
  current streak out of 10 days.

At the base tier those numbers add up to exactly the figure the FAQ quotes:
50 AE (milestone 1) + 10 posts × 10 AE + 50 AE (streak) = 200 AE.

**200 AE is not a cap.** There is no total-AE ceiling anywhere in
`superhero-api/src/profile/` — no constant, no env var, no check before a payout. The FAQ's
"up to 200 AE" is that base-tier arithmetic, nothing more. Structurally the program is
open-ended in two ways: the streak bonus is explicitly **recurring** (the source comment says
"every time the streak reaches the configured length it completes … and the counter resets,
so the user starts earning the next bonus from zero"), and the per-post ledger has no maximum
row count. What bounds your earnings is the rate limit in the next section, not a total.

## What the backend actually enforces

Everything above is what the deployed instance shows. The rules underneath it live in
`superhero-api/src/profile/profile.constants.ts` and
`src/profile/services/profile-x-posting-reward.service.ts`, and they are worth knowing
because most of the amounts are environment variables while the *structure* is fixed in code.

The structure is three independent payout paths, each with its own reward wallet and its own
enable flag:

| Path | Trigger | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Your first post containing a Superhero keyword | One-time. The threshold is the hard-coded constant `PROFILE_X_ONBOARDING_THRESHOLD = 1`. |
| Per-post | A post containing your referral link | Once per UTC day, at your follower tier's rate |
| Streak bonus | `PROFILE_X_REWARD_STREAK_LENGTH` consecutive UTC days of referral posts | Recurring — the counter resets and starts again |

The keywords that qualify a post for the onboarding path are hard-coded as
`superhero.com` and `superhero_chain`. The follower tiers are parsed from
`PROFILE_X_FOLLOWER_TIERS`, a comma-separated list of `minFollowers:amountAe` pairs sorted
ascending, with malformed or non-positive entries silently dropped. The minimum follower
count is `PROFILE_X_REWARD_MIN_FOLLOWERS`, and an account below it accrues nothing at all —
no qualifying posts, no per-post rewards, no streak progress — though already-accrued payouts
still settle.

One safety note for anyone reading the code: `PROFILE_REWARDS_DISABLED` defaults to `true`
and hard-overrides every per-reward toggle, so a freshly configured instance pays nothing
until it is explicitly armed. The code's own default amounts are placeholders (0.1 AE at the
lowest tier, for instance) and differ from the figures the live page shows; read the tiers off
the rewards card, not off the source.

### What counts as a qualifying post

- **Referral posts are matched host-pinned.** A URL in the post must have host
  `superhero.com` and a `?ref=` query parameter equal to your referral code,
  case-insensitively. A scheme-less fallback exists for X's truncated `display_url` values,
  but only when the candidate *starts* with the configured host followed by a `/`, `?`, `#`
  or end-of-string — so `evil.com/?u=superhero.com&ref=<code>` and
  `superhero.com.evil.com/?ref=<code>` are both rejected.
- **One rewarded post per UTC day.** The ledger has a unique index on
  `(x_user_id, tweet_utc_day)` with insert-or-ignore, so a second referral post on the same
  UTC day earns nothing. A tweet whose `created_at` cannot be parsed into a UTC day earns
  nothing either — the code fails closed rather than paying an uncappable row.
- **A streak day is a UTC day.** `updateStreak` collects the distinct UTC days of the
  referral posts found in this scan, walks them in order, and increments only when the day is
  exactly one after the last qualifying day. Any larger gap resets the streak to 1. So the
  streak is anchored to UTC midnight, not to your local time or to 24-hour spacing.
- **Old posts fall off the back.** A scan keeps the 2,000 newest tweets and advances a
  forward-only `since_id` cursor even when it truncates, so posts older than that page cap are
  never counted. The status response reports this as `x_posts_scan_truncated`.
- **Nothing is clawed back.** Payouts are on-chain AE spends from a reward wallet and the
  ledger is insert-only; the service has no path that reverses a paid row. Deleting a tweet
  after it has been rewarded stops it appearing in future scans but does not reclaim the AE.

## How verification works

Nothing here is on-chain until the payout. The flow is a signed request to the Superhero
backend, which scans X on your behalf.

1. The rewards card calls `GET /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward` on load and renders
   your status, rewarded post count, streak, follower count, tier and referral link.
2. Pressing **Check rewards** first requests a challenge from
   `POST /api/profile/x-posting-reward/recheck-challenge`. The response carries a `nonce`,
   an expiry and a `message`.
3. Your wallet signs that message. The signature, nonce and expiry are submitted to
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-posting-reward/recheck`, which runs the scan and returns
   your updated status.
4. Minting the referral link uses the same signed proof against
   `POST /api/profile/<address>/x-reward/referral-link`.

**The recheck is rate-limited.** The status response carries `next_check_allowed_at`; until
that timestamp passes the button is disabled and shows a countdown. A submit past the cap
returns HTTP 429 and the scan is skipped.

The window is `PROFILE_X_REWARD_DAILY_CAP_HOURS`, which defaults to 24 and falls back to 24
on bad input — so the client's "24 hours" is the default, not a constant. It is a hard cap on
paid X API reads, claimed atomically in the database so that retries, restarts and concurrent
requests cannot exceed it, and `next_check_allowed_at` is simply your last scan time plus that
many hours. Two details follow from the implementation:

- A *handled* scan failure — an X outage, say — still consumes the slot. That is deliberate:
  the cap protects the X API budget, so it fails closed. An *unexpected* failure such as a
  database error rolls the slot back so you are not locked out.
- Recovering a pending or failed payout never calls X, so it runs **before** the cap check.
  A recheck that finds a stuck payout can settle it even while the scan itself is cooling
  down.

**A successful check can still pay nothing.** A recheck that returns HTTP 200 may carry an
`error` string explaining why no reward was sent — below the follower minimum, the X identity
was already rewarded on another address, or the payout transaction failed. The card surfaces
that string as a red banner rather than silently reporting "no change".

**Payouts are automatic.** The earnings banner states plainly: "Your AE rewards have been
sent automatically to your connected wallet." There is no claim transaction for this program
and therefore no gas cost to you — and none exists to be added later without a new endpoint:
the reward controller exposes only status, challenge, recheck and referral-link routes.
Payment runs through `ProfileSpendQueueService`, which spends AE from a reward wallet held in
an environment variable and serialises spends per wallet so concurrent payouts cannot collide
on the account nonce. See [Claiming Rewards](/rewards/claiming) for the rewards that *do*
need a transaction.

## Eligibility — read this before participating

This is the disclaimer shown at the foot of the rewards page, reproduced in substance:

> The Superhero reward program rewards users for creating content on other social media
> platforms (like X) with backlinks and/or invite links, referring new users to the platform.
> Rewards will be distributed in the form of AE tokens. **Eligibility and rewards depend on
> on-chain activity, are not guaranteed and can be paused at any time without notice or
> liability.** The right is reserved to disqualify any user from the program. **Users from
> blacklisted countries are not eligible for rewards.** By participating in the program,
> users agree to these terms and conditions. Rewards sent may be subject to tax reporting.
> Users are responsible for any tax obligations arising from receiving rewards.

Concretely, that means:

- **Nothing is owed to you.** Completing a milestone does not create a claim.
- **The program can stop mid-stream.** It can be paused at any time, without notice, with no
  liability for work already done.
- **Geography matters.** Users in blacklisted countries are not eligible. The list is not
  published in the app, and it is not a check in the backend either — see the open question
  below.
- **Individual disqualification is allowed** at the operator's discretion.
- **Rewards are income.** They may be reportable, and the tax consequences are yours.

## Open questions

- **Which countries are blacklisted?** The disclaimer says users from blacklisted countries
  are not eligible, but there is no geographic check anywhere in the reward code — searching
  `superhero-api/src/` for country, geo, blacklist or `cf-ipcountry` turns up nothing outside
  a comment about reverse proxies. So it is not an application rule that a reader could look
  up; it is either enforced at the edge or applied manually when payouts are reviewed. Only
  Superhero's operators can publish the list.
