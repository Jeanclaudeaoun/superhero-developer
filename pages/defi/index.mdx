# DeFi & DEX

> **Page brief** — Document the DEX, and be explicit that it is a different market from the bonding curve. Readers mix them up.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

Superhero embeds a full AMM DEX at `/defi`. **This is separate from the bonding-curve token
market.** Bonding-curve community tokens are minted and burned against a curve; DEX tokens
trade against liquidity pools. Different contracts, different pricing, different risks.
