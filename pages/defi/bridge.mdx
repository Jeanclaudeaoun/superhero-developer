# Bridging from Ethereum

The in-app Ethereum bridge is disabled: `/defi/bridge` and `/defi/buy-ae-with-eth` both redirect
to `/defi/swap`.

## aeETH on the DEX

aeETH is the AEX-9 representation of bridged ETH on æternity. It remains a tradeable DEX asset:
if you hold aeETH, swap it at [`/defi/swap`](/defi/swap) like any other token, typically through
the aeETH/WAE pair.

| Network | aeETH contract |
| --- | --- |
| Mainnet | `ct_ryTY1mxqjCjq1yBn9i6HDaCSdA6thXUFZTA84EMzbWd1SLKdh` |
| Testnet | `ct_WVqAvLQpvZCgBg4faZLXA1YBj43Fxj91D33Z8K7pFsY8YCofv` |

aeETH is a claim on ETH held by the bridge on Ethereum, not ETH itself. [WAE](/defi/wrapped-ae),
by contrast, is backed by AE held in one contract on one chain.

## Bridging outside Superhero

æternity operates its own Ethereum bridge, documented on the
[æternity docs hub](https://docs.aeternity.com/aeternity-user-tools-and-services/aerc-bridge).
To get AE from ETH without bridging at all, use one of the swap aggregators listed under
[Buying AE](/defi/buy-ae).
