# Identity

> **Page brief** — Everything about who you are on Superhero — the ProfileRegistry contract, the three name types, X verification, and the W3C DID framing.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

Your identity on Superhero is your æternity address plus whatever you attach to it. The
`ProfileRegistry.aes` contract holds profiles and public names on-chain, so there is no
account system to be locked out of and no operator who can revoke your handle.
