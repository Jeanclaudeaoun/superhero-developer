# Official Links

Every official Superhero URL, shown as a literal string so you can compare it character by character
against whatever you were sent.

## The rule

> Superhero lives on `superhero.com` and `wallet.superhero.com`. Nothing else is the app.

Check the domain before you sign anything. A phishing site can copy the entire interface — the
palette, the logo, the copy — but it cannot copy the domain.

A "Superhero" token sale, airdrop, presale, migration or wallet-verification page on any domain
other than `superhero.com` and `wallet.superhero.com` is not Superhero.

**No one ever needs your key.** Not support, not a migration, not a verification, not an airdrop
claim, not a "wallet sync". Every legitimate Superhero action is a transaction you sign in your own
wallet. See [Security Best Practices](/wallets/security).
