# Brand & Media

The canonical brand kit is the in-app branding page at
[superhero.com/branding](https://superhero.com/branding), which serves every asset as a direct
download. This page mirrors the palette, typography and asset inventory so you can reference them
without opening the app, and states the licence position plainly — because it is more restrictive
than a download button implies.

## Colour palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Neon Teal | `#00ff9d` | `0, 255, 157` | Core interactions, success states, primary actions |
| Neon Pink / Crimson | `#ff6b6b` | `255, 107, 107` | Error states, destructive actions, active gradients |
| Neon Blue | `#0f61fe` | `15, 97, 254` | Sub-headings, secondary buttons, informative components |
| Background | `#0a0a0f` | `10, 10, 15` | The deep dark-mode backdrop the neon hues sit on |

The background colour is also the PWA's `theme_color` and `background_color` in
`manifest.webmanifest`, so it is what a mobile browser paints around the installed app. Superhero's
own description of the system: "Our deep dark-mode backdrop, optimized for high contrast with
radiant cyberpunk neon hues." There is no light theme.

## Typography

The primary typeface is **Inter**, described in the brand kit as "an open-source, modern type family
carefully crafted for high readability on screens."

```css
font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif;
```

Three weights carry the system: Regular for body, Semibold for emphasis, Black for headings. The
brand kit's own specimens are "Attention Markets" as a heading and "Follow Trends on-chain on a
decentralized network." as body text.

## Usage rules

Read this before you ship anything with the marks on it. The rules come from section V of the
[terms of use](https://superhero.com/terms), which is the operative document — the branding page's
download buttons do not grant a licence.

**What the terms say.** Logos, trademarks, domain names and code appearing on or accessible through
`https://superhero.com/` are protected intellectual property and belong "strictly and solely to
Superhero.com". Use of any of them is "subject to prior authorisation". None may be reproduced,
copied, edited, transmitted, downloaded or distributed — in whole or in part, on any medium —
without prior written consent.

**The two carve-outs.**

1. **Press.** Use "for press requirements is allowed solely in compliance with the intellectual
   property rights that are mentioned."
2. **Personal.** Copying is "authorised only for private, non-commercial, personal use on the
   personal technical devices of the user."

**In every permitted case**, the assets "must not be denatured, modified, altered or changed in any
way whatsoever." That rules out recolouring the logo, stretching it, adding your own wordmark to it,
or building a derivative mark from the shield.

**Practical guidance.** Editorial and press use of the unmodified logo is covered. Anything that
could read as endorsement — a product name, an app icon, a landing page, a token or a community that
presents itself as official — is not, and needs written permission first. Ask at
`superherowallet@protonmail.com`, the address the terms give for exactly this kind of question.

Three cases deserve a specific warning:

- **Do not use the marks on a site that collects keys or funds.** Superhero branding on a
  wallet-connect flow is the single most effective phishing pattern against this product. See
  [Official Links](/resources/links).
- **Community-run deployments are not official.** The terms note that because the interface is open
  source, "deployments may be operated independently by community members" and Superhero "is not
  responsible for the operation, modification, or compliance of third-party or community-run
  instances." If you run one, do not present it as Superhero.
- **Community tokens are not brand licences.** Creating a token named after Superhero, or using the
  logo as a token image, does not make it official and does not grant any trademark right.

## Writing about Superhero

A few conventions the product uses consistently, worth matching:

| Convention | Correct | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product name | Superhero | SuperHero, Super Hero |
| Domain | superhero.com | superhero.io, superhero.app |
| Chain | æternity, with the ligature | aeternity (acceptable in plain-ASCII contexts such as the FAQ copy) |
| Coin | AE | ÆTH, AET |
| Identity | Superhero Onchain ID, SuperheroID | Superhero ID (spaced) |
| Analytics surface | Trendminer | Trend Miner, TrendMiner |
| Wallet | Superhero Wallet — a separate product from the Superhero app | Treating `wallet.superhero.com` and `superhero.com` as the same thing |

Superhero's own one-line description, used in `manifest.webmanifest` and `llms.txt`: "Superhero is
an on-chain attention market built on the æternity blockchain where users can discover, trade, and
govern social trends."

## Open questions

All three need a brand document Superhero has not published; ask at
`superherowallet@protonmail.com` before relying on an assumption.

- **Is there a written brand licence separate from the terms of use?** The branding page presents
  assets as a "resource hub … to help you create materials", while section V of the terms requires
  prior written consent for any reproduction. No document reconciling the two appears in any of the
  six repositories searched, so the conservative reading above is the one to follow: treat the
  download button as convenience, not permission.
- **Are there approved logo clear-space, minimum-size or misuse rules?** The brand kit publishes
  colours, type and files but no layout specification, and nothing of the sort exists in
  `superhero/public/` — the shipped assets are `full-logo.svg`, `logo.png` and `og-default.png` with
  no accompanying guideline. A published brand guide would close this.
- **Is a light-background logo variant available?** Every asset in the kit is presented on the
  `#0a0a0f` background, `full-logo.svg` is the only vector shipped, and no inverse lockup exists in
  the repository. If you need one, ask rather than recolouring the SVG yourself — a modified mark is
  exactly what section V prohibits.
