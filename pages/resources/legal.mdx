# Terms & Privacy

The authoritative documents are the [terms of use](https://superhero.com/terms) and the
[privacy policy](https://superhero.com/privacy), both last updated **29 September 2025**. This page
does not restate them and is not legal advice. It points at the clauses that change what you should
do — particularly if you are building on Superhero or running an agent against it — and quotes them
where the exact wording matters.

The split the documents draw is between the **protocol** and the **Interface**. The protocol is
"an open-source, decentralised protocol deployed on the æternity blockchain" that "operates in a
distributed manner, and no single party controls or operates the underlying smart contracts." The
Interface is this website and its related surfaces, which the Company "provides and maintains … to
facilitate access to the protocol."

The consequence is stated directly: the Company "does not hold custody of assets, execute
transactions on behalf of users, or control the operation of the decentralised protocol." Everything
in [Architecture Overview](/concepts/architecture) is consistent with that — every write is a
transaction you sign.

## What the terms put on you

Five things, each of which has a practical counterpart in how you should use the product.

**Key custody is entirely yours.** You "remain solely responsible for securing your digital assets,
private keys, and compliance with applicable laws." There is no account recovery, because there is
no account. See [Backup & Recovery](/wallets/backup-and-recovery).

**The Interface is provided as is.** "The Website is provided 'as is' and 'as available,' without
warranties of any kind", and liability for "loss of funds, data, or profits" is disclaimed to the
fullest extent permitted by law. Nothing on the site "constitutes guidance or professional advice" —
including, by extension, the trending scores, charts and leaderboards.

**You indemnify broadly.** The indemnity covers your use of the Interface, "any transactions you
initiate, authorise, or execute via the blockchain using the Website", any breach of the terms or of
applicable law, failure to meet "tax, reporting, or regulatory obligations", and any content you
publish. It "will survive your use of the Website and termination of these Terms."

**Compliance is your problem.** "You are responsible for ensuring that your use of the Website
complies with the laws and regulations applicable to it." The documents do not enumerate restricted
jurisdictions for the app itself; the rewards programme does have its own restriction (below).

**Community deployments are not the operator's.** "As the Website is open-source, deployments may be
operated independently by community members. Superhero.com is not responsible for the operation,
modification, or compliance of third-party or community-run instances." A fork talking to the same
contracts is a different service with a different operator. See [Official Links](/resources/links).

Section V of the terms also governs the logos and marks; that is covered in
[Brand & Media](/resources/brand).

## What the privacy policy actually says

The policy is unusually short on collection because the architecture is unusual. Its three core
statements:

- "We do not collect, store, or process any personal data in connection with on-chain transactions."
- "Wallet addresses and transaction information recorded on the blockchain are not under our control
  and cannot be amended, deleted, or altered."
- "We do not track, log, or otherwise collect off-chain identifiers such as IP addresses, cookies,
  or analytics, unless explicitly stated elsewhere in this Privacy Policy."

And the two implications it draws itself, which matter more than the collection statements:

- Your activity "is pseudonymous, but may be considered personal data under GDPR if it can be linked
  to an identifiable individual."
- "Since we do not control the blockchain, we cannot modify or erase on-chain data, and we do not act
  as a data controller for such information."

**Read that as: erasure requests cannot reach your posts.** The right to be forgotten is listed among
your rights, and it applies to off-chain data — support correspondence, primarily — not to anything
in the Tipping contract. [On-Chain Social](/concepts/on-chain-social) covers what that means before
you post.

Off-chain data you volunteer, such as a support request, is processed on a consent basis under
Article 6(1) GDPR and retained "until the questions have been answered, assistance from the team has
been provided, the problems have been solved, or in any other case, until withdrawal of consent",
with a longer period where required by law. Disclosure to third parties is limited to enumerated
circumstances — legal process, investigating a possible crime, public authorities acting within
their mandate, a corporate transition, protecting rights or safety, or where otherwise required by
law. Data-subject requests go to `superherowallet@protonmail.com`.

## The rewards programme has its own terms

The Superhero Rewards Programme is governed separately from the terms of use. The FAQ says "you
agree to the terms shown on the rewards page", which reads as though a separate document exists —
**it does not.** The "terms shown on the rewards page" are a single paragraph of UI copy, stored at
`trending.inviteView.disclaimer` in `superhero/src/locales/en.json` and rendered on
`/trends/invite`. There is no standalone rewards agreement, no URL for one, and nothing longer to
read. In full, the programme reserves:

| Clause | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| "Eligibility and rewards depend on on-chain activity, are not guaranteed and can be paused at any time without notice or liability" | No accrued entitlement. A pause is not a breach |
| "The right is reserved to disqualify any user from the program" | Discretionary and unappealable, with no stated criteria |
| "Users from blacklisted countries are not eligible for rewards" | See the open questions — the list is nowhere |
| "Rewards will be distributed in the form of AE tokens" | Not fiat, not a stablecoin. Value moves with AE |
| "Rewards sent may be subject to tax reporting. Users are responsible for any tax obligations" | Yours to declare |

What the programme actually rewards is narrower than "activity": the same paragraph scopes it to
"creating content on other social media platforms (like X) with backlinks and/or invite links,
referring new users to the platform." The backend implements exactly that — a post qualifies when it
mentions `superhero.com` or `superhero_chain`
(`superhero-api/src/profile/profile.constants.ts`).

Claiming is an on-chain transaction and costs gas. See
[Superhero Rewards Program](/rewards/rewards-program) and [Claiming Rewards](/rewards/claiming).

## If you are building on Superhero

Nothing in the terms grants an API licence, a data licence or a brand licence, and none is published
separately. Concretely:

- **The read API is open, rate-limited, and covered by no terms of its own.** `api.superhero.com`
  requires no key, but the backend enforces 10 requests per minute per IP and route
  (`superhero-api/src/api-core/guards/rate-limit.guard.ts`). That limit is a code constant, not a
  published commitment: it can change in any deploy, so build as though it will.
- **Contract data is public and permissionless.** Reading contract state or events needs no
  permission from anyone, because the chain is public. Your product decisions about *displaying* it
  are yours.
- **Do not imply endorsement.** Brand use needs prior written consent; see
  [Brand & Media](/resources/brand).
- **Handle your users' keys the way the terms assume you will** — that is, not at all. Anything
  custodial takes you outside the model both documents describe.

## If you are running an agent

An AI agent signing with your key is you, legally. The indemnity covers "any transactions you
initiate, authorise, or execute via the blockchain using the Website", and an autonomous agent does
not create a third party to absorb that.

- **Autonomous mode is you trading.** Position sizes, entries and exits executed on a cron are
  transactions you authorised when you started the loop.
- **The agent scripts default to mainnet.** Every write script in the skill hard-codes
  `https://mainnet.aeternity.io`. There is no dry-run mode and no testnet default. See
  [Safety & Limits](/agents/safety).
- **Tax and reporting obligations do not pause for automation.** The indemnity names them explicitly.
- **The key is the whole security boundary.** Keep `AE_PRIVATE_KEY` in the environment, never in a
  file, never in a repository, never in a log. See [Wallet & Key Management](/agents/wallet-and-keys).

## Open questions

All three are questions for Superhero.com LVC at `superherowallet@protonmail.com`. They are legal
drafting questions, not engineering ones, and no amount of reading the code will answer them.

- **Which countries are excluded from the rewards programme?** The restriction is stated in the
  disclaimer above; the list is published nowhere. It is not a code-level control either — the
  backend has no country field, geo-IP lookup or jurisdiction check anywhere in `src/`, so the
  exclusion is applied by hand or not at all. Only Superhero can produce the list.
- **Do the terms cover token sales and DAOs?** Section II, "Subject of activity", describes a
  newsfeed of donations and comments plus access to token-swap, wrapping and liquidity contracts. It
  does not mention bonding-curve token sales, community DAOs or the ProtocolDAO token, which are the
  product's central mechanisms — and the contracts confirm those mechanisms are live, not planned.
  Whether they are intended to fall under the same section is not determinable from the document as
  published on 29 September 2025; it needs either an amended Section II or a written clarification.
- **Are there separate terms for the mobile apps or for Superhero Wallet?** The linked documents
  address "the Website" and the Interface. No app-store or wallet-specific agreement appears in any
  repository, including `superhero-com/superhero-wallet`. The app-store listings themselves are the
  next place to look.
