# Fund Your Wallet

AE is the native coin of the æternity blockchain. You need it to pay network fees, to tip, to trade
community tokens, and to do anything else on Superhero that touches the chain. This page covers
every way to get some, what each costs you in trust and time, and how much is actually enough to
start.

The app's own page for this is **[superhero.com/buy-ae](https://superhero.com/buy-ae)**, which
redirects to the canonical `/get-ae`. Everything below reflects what that page offers.

## How much you need

The FAQ's quick start says to fund your wallet with "a little AE", and that is honest — the costs
are small but they are not zero, and they are not all obvious.

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Publishing a post | Gas only. The agent skill's guide puts a post at roughly **0.00001 AE**. |
| Buying or selling a community token | The trade amount, plus fee and gas, plus a **1% curve spread** (0.5% to the community DAO, 0.5% to affiliation). |
| Claiming a `.chain` name | Gas plus a **name price**, which for desirable names dwarfs the fee. Superhero sponsors one free claim per address — see [Claim Your .chain Name](/getting-started/claim-name). |
| Voting in a DAO | Gas, plus an allowance transaction the first time. |

Two things that surprise people:

- **Gas is charged on top of the transaction fee**, up to the gas limit. A signing prompt that shows
  only "network fee" understates a contract call; Superhero's built-in wallet shows a separate
  "gas, at most" row for exactly this reason.
- **The trade screen's Max button does not offer your whole balance.** It withholds a slippage
  allowance plus a flat gas reserve:

  ```text
  max = balance − balance × (slippage + 1) / 100 − 0.001 AE
  ```

  At the default 1% slippage that is about 2% plus 0.001 AE. Fund with headroom or your first trade
  fails on arithmetic rather than on price.

**A working floor: 0.01 AE.** That is the balance the agent skill tells its agent to confirm before
doing anything on chain (`SKILL.md`), and it is the most defensible number available because
everything else is an estimate. One contract-call gas figure does come from a measurement: the
affiliation treasury's own source comments that redeeming an invitation costs "~2.01e14" aettos —
**0.000201 AE** — and the contract requires a fivefold over-provision of `10^15` aettos (0.001 AE) to
cover it (`bcl-contracts/contracts/AffiliationTreasury.aes`). So 0.01 AE is roughly fifty simple
contract calls' worth of room, before anything you actually want to spend.

Add to that whatever you intend to trade, plus the Max button's reserve above. Funding with **0.1 AE
or more** means you never think about any of this again for a first session.

## Earn it

The cheapest AE is AE you did not buy.

**Refer & Earn** — create invite links at [`/trends/invite`](https://superhero.com/trends/invite),
funded with AE, and share them. Someone who claims a link receives the funded reward. Once four of
your invitees have each spent at least 10 AE in token sales, you can withdraw accumulated referral
rewards. The affiliation system pays across four levels of the referral chain.

**The Superhero Rewards Program** — milestones for verifying your X account, linking it to your
Superhero ID on chain, and posting about Superhero with your referral link. The FAQ states these
unlock up to 200 AE. Eligibility depends on on-chain activity, is not guaranteed, and the program
can be paused at any time; blacklisted countries are excluded.

**Earn by posting on X** is listed on the get-AE page as **coming soon** and is disabled in the
current build.

Note the ordering problem: creating a funded invite link itself costs AE. Earning is an amplifier
once you are in, not a way to get your very first AE.

## Buy it

The get-AE page links four external providers. Superhero does not operate any of them and does not
custody anything on your behalf.

| Provider | What it offers |
| --- | --- |
| [Gate.io](https://www.gate.io/trade/AE_USDT) | An AE/USDT market — the main centralized-exchange route to AE liquidity |
| [Changelly](https://changelly.com/) | Many AE pairs, non-custodial swap flow |
| [Swapzone](https://swapzone.io/exchange/eth/ae) | Compares swap providers for an ETH to AE route |
| [SwapSpace](https://swapspace.co/?direction=direct&from=eth&fromNetwork=ETH&to=ae&toNetwork=AE) | ETH to AE with direct routing when available |

Availability and pairs change. Confirm details with the provider before sending anything.

**Superhero Wallet has a card-purchase entry too, and it is switched off in released builds.** Its
"Buy AE" panel item and dashboard card link a Simplex integration at `https://simplex.superhero.com`
(`AE_SIMPLEX_URL`), but both are rendered under `isNodeMainnet && UNFINISHED_FEATURES`, and
`.env.production` sets `UNFINISHED_FEATURES=false`. So you will not see it in the extension or the
app you installed from a store. That is the same pattern as superhero.com's disabled bridge and
buy-with-ETH routes below: the on-ramp code exists in both codebases and is turned off in both.

### Withdrawing to your own address

Whichever route you take, the end state you want is **native AE in your own `ak_...` address**, not a
balance on an exchange. When you withdraw:

- Confirm the **network** is æternity mainnet and the asset is native AE, not a wrapped
  representation on another chain. An AE token on Ethereum is not the same asset and cannot pay
  æternity fees.
- Paste the **full `ak_...` address** from your wallet. Copy it; do not retype it.
- Check the **withdrawal fee and minimum withdrawal amount** before you commit — a minimum above what
  you intended to buy is a common surprise.
- **Send a small test first**, then the rest. This is the app's own advice on the get-AE page and it
  is worth the extra fee.

## Bridge it

If you already hold ETH, bridging is the other route. The concept: bridge native ETH from Ethereum
to æternity as **æETH**, then swap æETH to AE on the Superhero DEX.

**Be aware of the current state of the build.** The dedicated bridge routes — `/defi/bridge` and
`/defi/buy-ae-with-eth` — are present in the router but their view components are commented out, so
both **redirect to `/defi/swap`**. The bridge widget's interface strings still ship in the app's
locale files. In other words: the feature exists in the codebase but is not reachable from those
URLs in this build.

If you already hold æETH on æternity, the swap half works normally on
[the DEX](https://superhero.com/defi/swap) — æETH has a deployed contract on both networks, listed
in [Using Testnet](/getting-started/testnet).

## Check that it arrived

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Your wallet | The balance directly |
| [aescan.io](https://aescan.io) | The transaction and your account's full history |
| An agent shell | `node scripts/superhero-wallet.mjs balance` |

Transactions confirm in a block; if a withdrawal has not appeared, check the exchange's withdrawal
status before assuming anything is wrong on chain.

## Safety, in three lines

The get-AE page's own warnings, which are the right ones:

1. Confirm the token, network, deposit address, withdrawal fees and minimum withdrawal amount.
2. Start with a small test transaction before moving larger amounts.
3. Keep custody in your own wallet where you can, and **never share private keys or seed phrases**.

## Open questions

- **Are the `/defi/bridge` and `/defi/buy-ae-with-eth` routes disabled temporarily or permanently?**
  The router keeps both the redirect and the commented-out view, which reads as "re-enable later",
  but nothing states the intent — and neither of the other two codebases resolves it. `superhero-api`
  has no bridge or on-ramp module at all, only the aeETH token address the DEX needs to price the
  pair; `superhero-wallet` keeps its own on-ramp (Simplex) behind an `UNFINISHED_FEATURES` flag that
  production builds set to `false`, which is consistent with "parked" but is evidence about a
  different product. Only a maintainer can say; ask on
  [the issue tracker](https://github.com/superhero-com/superhero/issues). See
  [Bridging from Ethereum](/defi/bridge).
- **Which exchanges besides Gate.io support AE withdrawal to a native `ak_` address?** The app links
  Gate.io plus three swap aggregators and nothing more; no list of withdrawal-capable exchanges
  exists in any repository, and it would go stale the moment it was written. Check the specific
  exchange's own withdrawal page for the æternity network before depositing, and test with a small
  amount.
- **What does a full first-post-plus-first-trade path actually cost, end to end?** The 0.01 AE floor
  above is a working answer, not a measurement. Capturing the real figure means running both actions
  on mainnet and summing the fees and gas off the two transactions.
