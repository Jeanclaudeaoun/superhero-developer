# Fund Your Wallet

AE is the native coin of the æternity blockchain. You need it to pay network fees, to tip, to trade
community tokens, and to do anything else on Superhero that touches the chain.

The app's own page for this is **[superhero.com/buy-ae](https://superhero.com/buy-ae)**, which
redirects to `/get-ae`.

## How much you need

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Publishing a post | Gas only — roughly **0.00001 AE**. |
| Buying or selling a community token | The trade amount, plus fee and gas, plus a **1% curve spread** (0.5% to the community DAO, 0.5% to affiliation). |
| Claiming a `.chain` name | Gas plus a **name price**, which for short names dwarfs the fee. Superhero sponsors one free claim per address — see [Claim Your .chain Name](/getting-started/claim-name). |
| Voting in a DAO | Gas, plus an allowance transaction the first time. |

Two things that surprise people:

- **Gas is charged on top of the transaction fee**, up to the gas limit. Superhero's built-in wallet
  shows a separate "gas, at most" row for this reason.
- **The trade screen's Max button does not offer your whole balance.** It withholds a slippage
  allowance plus a flat gas reserve:

  ```text
  max = balance − balance × (slippage + 1) / 100 − 0.001 AE
  ```

  At the default 1% slippage that is about 2% plus 0.001 AE. Fund with headroom or your first trade
  fails on arithmetic rather than on price.

**A working floor: 0.01 AE.** That is the balance the agent skill confirms before doing anything on
chain. For scale, redeeming an invitation costs about **0.000201 AE** in gas, and the contract
requires 0.001 AE attached to cover it — so 0.01 AE is roughly fifty simple contract calls' worth of
room.

Add whatever you intend to trade, plus the Max button's reserve. Funding with **0.1 AE or more**
covers a first session without further thought.

## Earn it

**Refer & Earn** — create invite links at [`/trends/invite`](https://superhero.com/trends/invite),
funded with AE, and share them. Someone who claims a link receives the funded reward. Once four of
your invitees have each spent at least 10 AE in token sales, you can withdraw accumulated referral
rewards. The affiliation system pays across four levels of the referral chain.

**The Superhero Rewards Program** — milestones for verifying your X account, linking it to your
Superhero ID on chain, and posting about Superhero with your referral link, unlocking up to 200 AE.
Eligibility depends on on-chain activity and the program can be paused.

**Earn by posting on X** is live. Link your X account and make one qualifying post about Superhero
and the onboarding reward pays **50 AE**. See [Verifying Your X Account](/identity/x-verification)
for the linking flow and the [rewards program](/rewards/rewards-program) for what follows it.

Creating a funded invite link itself costs AE, so earning is an amplifier once you are in, not a
way to get your very first AE.

## Buy it

The get-AE page links one external provider. Superhero does not operate it and does not custody
anything on your behalf.

| Provider | What it offers |
| --- | --- |
| [Gate.io](https://www.gate.io/trade/AE_USDT) | An AE/USDT market, and the only route currently confirmed working |

The instant-swap and aggregator routes that used to be listed here are gone. They no longer
complete an AE purchase, so following them wasted people's time.

Availability and pairs change. Confirm details with the provider before sending anything.
Superhero Wallet's released builds do not include a card-purchase route.

### Withdraw to your own address

The end state you want is **native AE in your own `ak_...` address**, not a balance on an
exchange. When you withdraw:

- Confirm the **network** is æternity mainnet and the asset is native AE. An AE token on Ethereum
  is a different asset and cannot pay æternity fees.
- Paste the **full `ak_...` address** from your wallet. Copy it; do not retype it.
- Check the **withdrawal fee and minimum withdrawal amount** before you commit.
- **Send a small test first**, then the rest.

## Bridge it

If you already hold ETH, bridge native ETH from Ethereum to æternity as **æETH**, then swap æETH to
AE on the Superhero DEX.

The dedicated routes `/defi/bridge` and `/defi/buy-ae-with-eth` currently **redirect to
`/defi/swap`**. If you already hold æETH on æternity, the swap half works on
[the DEX](https://superhero.com/defi/swap) — æETH has a deployed contract on both networks, listed
in [Using Testnet](/getting-started/testnet).

## Check that it arrived

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Your wallet | The balance |
| [aescan.io](https://aescan.io) | The transaction and your account's full history |
| An agent shell | `node scripts/superhero-wallet.mjs balance` |

Transactions confirm in a block. If a withdrawal has not appeared, check the exchange's withdrawal
status before assuming anything is wrong on chain.

## Safety

1. Confirm the token, network, deposit address, withdrawal fee and minimum withdrawal amount.
2. Start with a small test transaction before moving larger amounts.
3. Keep custody in your own wallet, and **never share private keys or seed phrases**.
