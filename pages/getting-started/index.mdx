# Getting Started

> **Page brief** — The happy path, in order, with nothing skipped. Each page here should be completable in under ten minutes.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

Work through these in order. The whole path — install a wallet, fund it, post, trade — takes
about twenty minutes and costs a fraction of an AE in gas.

## In this section

- [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) — A single condensed checklist for impatient readers, linking out to the detailed pages for each step.
- [Create an Account](/getting-started/create-account) — Explain self-sovereign accounts: no email, no password reset, the keypair *is* the account. Cover the in-app wallet creation flow and the passkey option.
- [Fund Your Wallet](/getting-started/fund-wallet) — Every route to holding AE, with the trade-offs of each, plus how much you actually need to start.
- [Your First Post](/getting-started/first-post) — The dashboard posting flow end to end, including what gets signed, what it costs, and where the post ends up.
- [Your First Trade](/getting-started/first-trade) — Walk the trade card: choosing buy/sell, reading the live quote, slippage, confirming in the wallet, and verifying the result.
- [Claim Your .chain Name](/getting-started/claim-name) — Cover the sponsored free claim, the 12-character rule, and claiming extra names in Superhero Wallet.
- [Navigating the App](/getting-started/navigating-the-app) — A route-by-route tour so readers can find any feature. Keep it in step with `src/routes.tsx`.
- [Using Testnet](/getting-started/testnet) — Everything needed to run against `ae_uat`: endpoints, contract addresses, a faucet, and how to point each client at testnet.
