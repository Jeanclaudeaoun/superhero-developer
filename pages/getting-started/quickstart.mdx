# Quickstart

> **Page brief** — A single condensed checklist for impatient readers, linking out to the detailed pages for each step.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

The FAQ's own quick start, which this page should expand:

1. Install Superhero Wallet and fund it with a little AE.
2. Open **Trending** and sort by Market Cap to see established communities.
3. Tap a token, connect your wallet, and buy a small amount to try it.
4. Join the token's DAO and vote on proposals when available.
