# Tipping

> **Page brief** — Document the tipping flow, where the AE goes, and how tip totals are aggregated and displayed.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

Tip summaries are served from `GET /api/tips/posts/{postId}/summary`, which returns
`totalTips`. Per-account summaries come from `GET /api/tips/accounts/{address}/summary`.
