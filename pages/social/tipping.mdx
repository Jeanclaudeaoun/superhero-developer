# Tipping

Tipping on Superhero is a plain AE transfer with a payload that names the post. Despite the
content contract being called `Tipping`, the tip itself is not a contract call: it is a spend
transaction from your account straight to the author's, and the indexer reads the payload to
attribute it. That design means no fee, no escrow, and no intermediary that could fail to pay
out.

## What actually happens

```text
sdk.spend(amountInAettos, recipientAddress, { payload })
```

| Part | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Amount | The AE amount converted to aettos, 18 decimals |
| Recipient | The post author's `ak_…` address, directly |
| Payload | `TIP_POST:` plus the post id with its `_v3` suffix |
| Payload for a profile tip | `TIP_PROFILE` |
| Encoding | The payload string is byte-array encoded, arriving on chain as a `ba_…` value |

The post id is normalized before it goes into the payload: an id without `_v3` gets the suffix
appended, so `TIP_POST:3903_v3` is the canonical form regardless of how the caller spelled it.

Because it is an ordinary spend, the AE lands in the author's balance in the same transaction.
There is nothing to claim, no vesting, and no minimum enforced by the protocol.

## Why the Tipping contract is not involved

Tipping v3's entire entrypoint surface is `init`, `post_without_tip`, `post_without_tip_sig`
and `get_state`. There is no tip entrypoint, no payable function and no balance held by the
contract. The name is historical: the contract is the content layer, and tipping migrated to
plain transfers.

Practical consequences:

- A tip cannot fail because of contract logic; it fails only if you cannot pay.
- A tip is attributable off-chain, by the indexer reading the payload. If the indexer misses
  it, the AE still arrived — only the displayed total is wrong.
- Anyone can reconstruct tip attribution themselves by scanning spend transactions to an
  address and decoding payloads that start with `TIP_POST:`.

## Tipping from the app

The tip button sits on every post in the feed and on the post page. It shows the post's current
total in AE when there is one, and the word Tip when there is not.

1. Click Tip on a post. The modal opens with the recipient resolved to the author's display
   name.
2. Enter an amount, or use a quick amount: 1, 10, 100 or 500 AE.
3. Confirm in your wallet. The modal closes before the wallet opens so the two do not stack.

The button then shows Sending, and Tipped once the transaction returns. Your balance is checked
client-side before the send, so an amount above your balance disables the button rather than
failing in the wallet.

### Why the total takes a moment

The displayed total comes from the indexer, which has to see the transaction confirm before it
can update. The app bridges the gap:

- It optimistically adds your amount to the cached total immediately.
- It then polls `GET /api/tips/posts/{postId}/summary` — 5 seconds after the send, then every
  3 seconds for up to 18 more attempts, roughly a minute in total.
- It stops as soon as the backend total reaches or exceeds the expected value.

If the minute elapses without the backend catching up, the app does a final refetch and leaves
the real value in place. Your optimistic bump is display only; it is never written anywhere.

## Reading tip totals

| Endpoint | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/tips/posts/{postId}/summary` | `totalTips` — the post's total, a string the client reads as a number of AE |
| `GET /api/tips/accounts/{address}/summary` | Defined in the generated client, with no callers in the app |

The post id in the path takes the `_v3` suffix, matching the payload form.

Tip signals also feed the popular feed: `tipsAmountAE`, `tipsCount` and `uniqueTippers` are
three of the eight weighting inputs a reader can scale. See [The Feed](/social/feed).

## Tipping someone who has not claimed anything

There is nothing to claim. The recipient is an æternity address, and an address exists whether
or not anyone has ever opened it in a wallet. A tip to an address whose owner has never used
Superhero simply increases that address's balance; if the key is lost, so is the AE. There is
no pending-tip pool and no reclaim path.

## Open questions

- **Is there a minimum tip?** None is enforced in the modal beyond requiring a positive amount
  within your balance, and none can be enforced on chain since the tip is a plain transfer.
  Whether the indexer ignores dust is not documented.
- **Does the indexer count tips sent outside the app?** A manually constructed spend with a
  `TIP_POST:` payload should be indistinguishable on chain from one the app sent, but no source
  confirms the indexer accepts them.
- **What does the account tip summary return?** `GET /api/tips/accounts/{address}/summary` is
  defined but unused, and its response shape is not recorded in the REST inventory.
- **Are tips shown on profiles?** The per-account endpoint exists but nothing in the client
  calls it, so there is no account-level tip surface today.
