# Hashtags & Topics

> **Page brief** — The bridge page between the social layer and the token layer. This is the mechanism the whole product rests on.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

The landing page states the equivalence directly: **Trends = Hashtags = Communities = Tokens.**

A hashtag in a post becomes a topic with its own feed (`GET /api/topics/name/{name}` returns
`posts` and `post_count`). Trending tags are served from `GET /api/trending-tags` with
`order_by`, `order_direction`, `limit`, `page` and `search` parameters, returning items with a
`tag`/`name`, a `score`/`value`, and a `source`/`platform`.

When a topic has a token, the hashtag in a post links straight to the token page.
