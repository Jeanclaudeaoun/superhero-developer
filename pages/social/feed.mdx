# The Feed

> **Page brief** — Explain the popular/latest feeds, what drives ranking, and how the infinite feed pages through the API.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

The feed is served from `GET /api/posts/popular` with `window`, `page` and `limit` query
parameters, returning `items` plus a `meta` block (`currentPage`, `totalPages`, `totalItems`,
`itemCount`). The popular feed is behind the `POPULAR_FEED_ENABLED` config flag.
