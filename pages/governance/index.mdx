# Governance & Voting

> **Page brief** — Document the protocol-level governance system, which is separate from per-community DAOs. Make that distinction loud — people confuse the two constantly.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

**This is not the same thing as a community DAO.** Community DAOs govern one token's
treasury. The governance polls documented here are æternity-wide, stake-weighted polls run
through a `Registry` contract, surfaced in Superhero at `/voting`.

The Voting page is a single interface with tabs for browsing polls, voting, managing
delegations, and viewing your on-chain governance activity.

## In this section

- [Polls](/governance/polls) — Explain the poll data model and — critically — which result figure is authoritative.
- [Creating a Poll](/governance/create-a-poll) — The creation guide. It must front-load the constraints, because a poll is two transactions and cannot be edited afterwards.
- [Voting on Polls](/governance/voting) — Short and procedural. Emphasise reading the poll before voting so option numbers are right.
- [Delegation](/governance/delegation) — Document the delegation model in the Registry contract and what it means for stake weighting.
- [The Governance Registry](/governance/registry) — A contract reference for the Registry, including its deployed addresses.
