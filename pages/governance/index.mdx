# Governance & Voting

æternity's protocol-level governance runs stake-weighted polls through a `Registry` contract,
with each poll deployed as its own `Poll` contract and weighted by AE balance and delegation.
Superhero surfaces the whole system at [superhero.com/voting](https://superhero.com/voting).

## Not the same thing as a community DAO

Two different systems, two different contract sets, two different sources of voting power.

| | æternity governance poll | Community DAO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contracts | `Registry.aes`, `Poll.aes` | `DAO.aes`, `DAOVote.aes` |
| Scope | The æternity ecosystem as a whole | One community token's treasury |
| Voting power | AE balance at the poll's close height, plus delegated AE | That community's AEX-9 token balance |
| Tokens locked while voting | **No** | **Yes** — an AEX-9 allowance locks your stake |
| Delegation | Yes, global and transitive | No |
| Ballot | Any list of at least two options | Binary: in favour or against |
| Outcome | Signalling. Nothing executes automatically. | Executes an on-chain action via `apply_vote_subject` |
| Cost to open | Two transactions, roughly 0.02 AE | 555 AE, non-refundable |
| Duration | Whatever close height the author sets, or never | Fixed at two weeks |
| Where in the app | `/voting` | `/trends/daos` |

**A governance poll does not lock your tokens and does not execute anything.** It records
stake-weighted opinion at a block height; acting on that result is an off-chain process. A
community DAO vote locks your stake and, if it passes, moves real money on-chain. For
community treasuries and payout proposals, see [Community DAOs](/dao).

## How a poll works

1. Somebody deploys a `Poll` contract with a title, a description, a reference link and a list
   of options, then registers it in the `Registry` — two transactions.
2. Any account votes by calling `vote(option)` on the poll contract. One vote per address; you
   can change it or revoke it at any time before the poll closes.
3. Accounts that have not voted have their AE counted for whoever they delegated to.
4. At the poll's close height, balances and delegations **at that height** decide the weight of
   each option.
5. The governance backend computes and publishes the stake-weighted result.

**The official result is `stake_weighted_results`.** Raw vote counts are address counts and
are not the outcome — see [Polls](/governance/polls).

## The Voting page

Superhero's `/voting` route is one page with four tabs, driven by the URL:

| Tab | Route | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polls | `/voting` | Browse and search polls, filtered by open, closed or all |
| Vote | `/voting/p/:pollAddress` | One poll: its options, live results, and your vote |
| My Account | `/voting/account` | Your balance, your delegation, and who delegates to you |
| Create Poll | `/voting/create` | The poll creation form |
