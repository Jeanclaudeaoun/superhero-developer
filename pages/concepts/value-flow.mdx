# Where the Money Goes

> **Page brief** — Follow one purchase end to end and account for every fraction of it. This is the page people will link to when they ask 'what are the fees'.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

On every community-token **buy**:

```text
Buyer pays AE
  ├── 99.0%  → Bonding curve reserve (redeemable by sellers)
  ├──  0.5%  → Community DAO treasury (governed by token holders)
  └──  0.5%  → Affiliation system
                ├── 0.300% → Level 1 referrer
                ├── 0.125% → Level 2 referrer
                ├── 0.050% → Level 3 referrer
                └── 0.025% → Level 4 referrer
                (if no referrer: burned)

Buyer also receives:
  └── ProtocolDAO tokens = 1,000 × AE value of the buy
```

The 1% total spread is the difference between the buy curve and the sell curve. It is not
charged as a separate fee — it is built into the two curves. See
[Bonding Curves](/tokens/bonding-curve) for the math.
