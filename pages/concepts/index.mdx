# Core Concepts

You have been early before. A song, a meme, a project, a community — you saw it before it broke
out, you told people, you helped it happen. Then the platform sold ads against the attention you
created, and you got a badge.

**Superhero is built on the idea that being early should be worth something.**

So a post here belongs to the person who wrote it, not to a database someone else controls. A
trending topic is not just a hashtag — it is a market you can back while it is still small, priced
by nothing but how many people have arrived before you. And the fees that trading generates flow
into a treasury the community votes on, instead of out to an advertiser.

Attention, priced in the open, owned by the people who make it. That is the whole idea.

These pages explain how it actually works underneath — including the sharp edges, because there
are some. If you already know what a bonding curve is, skip ahead to
[Where the Money Goes](/concepts/value-flow); it answers the question everyone asks first.

## Three mechanisms, and you understand the whole thing

Strip away the interface and Superhero runs on three ideas. Almost every question about the
product is really a question about one of them.

**Posts are contract calls.** A post is a `post_without_tip` call on the `Tipping.aes` v3 contract.
The author's address, the text and a list of media URLs are written into contract state and indexed
by a `tip_id`. Nobody — including Superhero — can delete it afterwards.

**Topics are tokens.** Any hashtag can be turned into an AEX-9 token whose price is a deterministic
function of its own circulating supply. There is no order book and no counterparty: you buy from the
curve and sell back to it, and the contract holds a reserve large enough to redeem the entire supply.

**The spread funds the community.** The gap between the buy curve and the sell curve is 1%. Half
goes to the token's own DAO treasury, half to a four-level referral chain. Token holders vote on
what the treasury does with it.
