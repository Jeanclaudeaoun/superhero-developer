# Attention Markets

> **Page brief** — Explain the core thesis: trends are the asset, and a bonding curve turns conviction into a price without an order book.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

The claim Superhero makes is that **attention is already a market — it just has no price feed.**
Hashtags trend, communities form, and value accrues to platforms rather than participants.
Superhero gives every trend a token whose price is a deterministic function of how many
people have bought in.

Key consequences to develop on this page:

- **No order book, no counterparty.** You always trade against the curve, so there is
  always liquidity and always a quotable price.
- **Price is a momentum signal.** Because supply only moves when someone buys or sells,
  price and supply encode net conviction.
- **The social loop.** Posting about a topic drives attention, attention drives buying
  pressure, buying pressure moves the curve. This loop is the product.
