### What is Superhero?

A social + crypto app on the æternity blockchain. Discover content, create posts, tip, trade
community tokens, and take part in on-chain governance — all in one place.

### What is Trendminer?

Trendminer lets you tokenize trending ideas. Creators launch community tokens on a bonding
curve; price adjusts with buys and sells, with no order book. A small fee flows into a token
treasury (DAO) to fund community initiatives.

### How do I buy or sell a token?

On a token page, use the trade card. Choose Buy or Sell, review the live quote, and confirm in
your wallet. Tokens are minted or burned against AE along the bonding curve.

### Where do fees go?

A portion of each trade funds the token's DAO treasury. Holders propose and vote on how to
allocate it.

### How does the DAO work?

Every token has a treasury. Anyone can create proposals. Holders vote on-chain. If a proposal
passes, the treasury can pay contributors or fund new ideas.

### Which wallet do I need?

Superhero Wallet for æternity — browser extension, mobile app, or the web wallet at
`wallet.superhero.com`. See [Wallets](/wallets).

### What is a sponsored name claim?

Superhero sponsors one free `.chain` name claim per address. The name must be longer than
12 characters (excluding the `.chain` suffix). Additional names can be claimed directly in
Superhero Wallet.

### Any risks I should know?

Volatility and smart-contract risk are real. Double-check addresses and amounts before
signing. Never risk funds you cannot afford to lose.
