# Frequently Asked Questions

The 23 questions below are the canonical user FAQ, reproduced in the order and wording used on
[superhero.com/faq](https://superhero.com/faq). After them come a developer FAQ and an agent FAQ
that do not exist in the app, built from the repositories.

The canonical copy lives in `superhero/server/lib/faq-content.cjs` and the `faq` namespace of
`src/locales/en.json`. **Keep this section in step with that file** — it is the source of the
schema.org structured data Google reads, and the ordering below follows `QUESTION_DEFS` in
`src/views/FAQ.tsx`.

## User FAQ

### 1. What is Superhero?

Superhero is a social + crypto app on the aeternity blockchain. You can discover content, create
posts, tip, trade community tokens, and take part in on‑chain governance — all in one place.

*Overview · More in [What is Superhero?](/concepts/what-is-superhero)*

### 2. Who powers chat here?

We partner with Quali.chat — a quality chat app for crypto people. Expect realtime conversations,
moderation tools, and crypto‑friendly UX. Each token has a public room; holders can coordinate faster.

*Chat & Community · More in [Chat](/social/chat)*

### 3. What is Trendminer?

Trendminer lets you tokenize trending ideas. Creators launch community tokens on a bonding curve.
Price adjusts with buys and sells; no order books, just math. A small fee flows into a token
treasury (DAO) to fund community initiatives.

*Overview · More in [Attention Markets](/concepts/attention-markets)*

### 4. Quick start: how do I begin?

Install Superhero Wallet and fund it with a little AE. Open Trending and sort by Market Cap to see
established communities. Tap a token, connect wallet, and buy a small amount to try it. Join the
token's DAO and vote on proposals when available.

*Getting Started · More in [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart)*

### 5. How do I buy or sell a token?

On a token page, use the trade card. Choose Buy or Sell, review the live quote, and confirm in your
wallet. Tokens are minted/burned against AE along the bonding curve.

*Trading · More in [Buying & Selling](/tokens/buying-and-selling)*

### 6. Where do fees go?

A portion of each trade funds the token's DAO treasury. Holders can propose and vote to allocate
funds for growth, bounties, or community rewards.

*Trading · More in [Where the Money Goes](/concepts/value-flow)*

### 7. What are Holders and Transactions tabs?

Holders shows top addresses and balances for the token. Transactions lists recent buys/sells with
timestamps and transaction hashes.

*Trading · More in [Reading a Token Page](/tokens/token-page)*

### 8. How does the DAO work?

Every token has a treasury. Anyone can create proposals. Holders vote on‑chain. If a proposal
passes, the treasury can pay contributors or fund new ideas.

*DAO & Governance · More in [How DAOs Work](/dao/overview)*

### 9. What is the Voting section?

The Voting page provides a unified interface for browsing polls, voting, managing delegations, and
viewing your on‑chain governance activity. All governance functions are accessible through tabs
within a single page.

*DAO & Governance · More in [Governance & Voting](/governance)*

### 10. Is there a social feed?

Yes. You can browse posts, comment, and explore communities. Comments and token chats are powered by
Quali.chat — real‑time rooms built for crypto‑native communities.

*Social & Feed · More in [The Feed](/social/feed)*

### 11. What can I see on Accounts pages?

Account profiles show owned tokens, created tokens, and activity. Leaderboards highlight the most
active creators and traders.

*Accounts & Rankings · More in [Profiles & Accounts](/identity/profiles) and [Leaderboards](/rewards/leaderboards)*

### 12. What is the Superhero Rewards Program?

The Superhero Rewards Program lets you earn AE tokens by completing milestones: verifying your X
account, inviting new users, and posting about Superhero on social media. Complete milestones to
unlock phases and earn up to 200 AE tokens.

*Rewards Program · More in [Superhero Rewards Program](/rewards/rewards-program)*

### 13. What milestones are available?

Verify X Account & Post — connect your X account and post to prove ownership. Link your X account to
SuperheroID on-chain. Post about Superhero — publish 10 posts that include your referral link in the
text.

*Rewards Program · More in [Superhero Rewards Program](/rewards/rewards-program)*

### 14. How do I claim my rewards?

Once a milestone is marked as completed, a claim banner appears on the Rewards page. Click the claim
button and confirm the transaction in your wallet. A small gas fee applies.

*Rewards Program · More in [Claiming Rewards](/rewards/claiming)*

### 15. Are rewards guaranteed?

Eligibility and rewards depend on on-chain activity and are not guaranteed. The program can be
paused at any time without notice. Users from blacklisted countries are not eligible. By
participating, you agree to the terms shown on the rewards page.

*Rewards Program · More in [Terms & Privacy](/resources/legal)*

### 16. What are invitations?

The Trading Affiliate Program lets you create invite links funded with AE. When someone claims a
link they receive the funded reward. Once 4 of your invitees each spend at least 10 AE in token
sales, you can withdraw accumulated referral rewards.

*Invites & Rewards · More in [Invite Links](/rewards/invite-links)*

### 17. How is Market Cap and pricing shown?

We display price and market cap using AE as the unit. Trending defaults to Market Cap so larger
communities surface first. Token pages include candlestick charts, recent transactions, and holders
to help you read the market.

*Pricing & Analytics · More in [Reading a Token Page](/tokens/token-page)*

### 18. Which wallet do I need?

Use Superhero Wallet for aeternity. Install it, create or import your account, then connect on token
pages to trade and participate.

*Wallet & Security · More in [Choosing a Wallet](/wallets/choosing-a-wallet)*

### 19. Any risks I should know?

Volatility and smart‑contract risk are real. Double‑check addresses and amounts before signing.
Never risk funds you cannot afford to lose.

*Wallet & Security · More in [Risks](/tokens/risks)*

### 20. Where do I get help or report bugs?

Reach out via official channels listed in the app. Include the token name, your address, the tx
hash, and what happened.

*Support · More in [Support](/resources/support)*

### 21. What is a sponsored name claim?

We sponsor one free .chain name claim per address. The name must be longer than 12 characters
(excluding the .chain suffix). This gives you a human‑readable identity on the æternity blockchain
at no cost.

*Name Claims · More in [Claim Your .chain Name](/getting-started/claim-name)*

### 22. Can I claim more than one name?

We only sponsor one name claim per address. If you'd like to claim additional names, you can do so
directly in Superhero Wallet — available as a browser extension, native mobile app, or on the web at
wallet.superhero.com.

*Name Claims · More in [Chain Names (AENS)](/identity/chain-names)*

### 23. Glossary: AE, sale address, DAO, tx hash

AE: the native coin of the aeternity blockchain. Sale address: the contract that mints/burns tokens
on the curve. DAO: on‑chain treasury + governance for a token community. Tx hash: the transaction
ID; view in explorers to verify.

*Glossary · More in [Glossary](/concepts/glossary)*

## Developer FAQ

These answers are not in the app. They come from the repositories and are cited so you can check them.

### Where do I find the contract addresses?

In `superhero/src/config.ts`, per network, and mirrored in [Deployed Addresses](/contracts/addresses).
The Tipping v3 content contract is `ct_2Hyt9ZxzXra5NAzhePkRsDPDWppoatVD7CtHnUoHVbuehwR8Nb` on
mainnet and `ct_2J1wuuw9urs9ADBh5QbvuPyUCLdKbW5YRkfhgPoN7rGjBbPiBW` on testnet. Community token
contracts are **not** in config — they are deployed per community, so you look up a token's
`sale_address` through `GET /api/tokens` or the active factory at `GET /api/factory`.

### What is the API base URL?

`https://api.superhero.com` on mainnet, `https://testnet.api.dev.tokensale.org` on testnet. The node
and middleware are separate: `https://mdw.wordcraft.fun` and `https://mdw.wordcraft.fun/mdw` on
mainnet, `https://testnet.aeternity.io` and `https://testnet.aeternity.io/mdw` on testnet. See
[Conventions](/api/conventions).

### Is the API authenticated? Are there rate limits?

Every read call in the agent skill is a plain `fetch` with no authorization header, so the read API
is open. Writes are not API calls at all — they are signed transactions you send to the node. Profile
mutation is the exception: it goes through a challenge-and-signature flow
(`POST /api/profile/[address]/challenge`, then `PATCH`). No rate limit is documented anywhere in
these repositories — see the open questions.

### How do I run against testnet?

The web app branches at build time on `VITE_NETWORK`; set it to `ae_uat` for testnet, anything else
gives mainnet. A deployed container can also override the endpoints at runtime through a
`window.__SUPERCONFIG__` object served by the server. Note that the agent skill's scripts do **not**
read `VITE_NETWORK` — see the agent FAQ below. Full walkthrough in [Using Testnet](/getting-started/testnet).

### Where do I get test AE?

From the æternity testnet faucet at [faucet.aepps.com](https://faucet.aepps.com/), which takes a
wallet address and nothing else. Testnet AE has no value and is only good against
`ae_uat`. See [Testnets and Faucet](https://docs.aeternity.com/aeternity-developer-tools/development-infrastructure/testing-and-deployment/testnets-and-faucet).

### Do I need to compile the contracts to call them?

No. The agent skill ships compiled ACIs under `contracts/` — `Tipping_v3.aci.json`,
`AffiliationBondingCurveTokenSale.aci.json`, `BondingCurveExponential.aci.json`,
`CommunityFactory.aci.json`, `FungibleTokenFull.aci.json` and the governance ACIs. Hand one to
`Contract.initialize` from `@aeternity/aepp-sdk` together with the contract address. A compiler is
configured (`https://v7.compiler.aepps.com`) but is only needed if you are deploying your own Sophia
source. See [Calling Contracts](/contracts/calling-contracts).

### Why does selling need two transactions?

AEX-9 has no transfer-from without prior approval. You call `create_allowance` (or
`change_allowance`) on the community token to let the sale contract move your tokens, then
`sell(count, minimal_return_required)` on the sale contract. Buying is one transaction because you
are sending AE, not tokens. See [Where the Money Goes](/concepts/value-flow).

### What is a post ID, exactly?

The contract returns an integer `tip_id`. The clients and the API use that integer with a `_v3`
suffix, so a post published as `tip_id` 12352 is addressed as `12352_v3` and shown at a slug URL
like `https://superhero.com/post/ever-notice-crypto-communities-12352`. A comment is an ordinary
post whose first media entry is `comment:PARENT_ID`. See [Posts](/api/posts).

### How do I get live updates instead of polling?

Open a socket.io connection to the API origin and subscribe to `token-created`, `token-updated`,
`token-transaction` or `token-history`. Two of them fan out per `sale_address`. See
[WebSocket](/api/websocket).

### Which SDK should I use?

[`@aeternity/aepp-sdk`](https://github.com/aeternity/aepp-sdk-js) for JavaScript and TypeScript. Both
the web app and the agent skill use it, along with `bignumber.js` for amounts — never use JavaScript
numbers for aettos.

### How do I verify something actually happened?

Look the transaction hash up in an explorer: [aescan.io](https://aescan.io) for mainnet,
[testnet.aescan.io](https://testnet.aescan.io) for testnet. Contract state is readable directly too —
`get_state()` on the Tipping contract returns every post ever published. See [Explorers](/aeternity/explorers).

## Agent FAQ

For AI agents driving Superhero through the
[agent skill](https://github.com/superhero-com/superhero-agent-skill).

### How does the agent hold a key?

In the `AE_PRIVATE_KEY` environment variable, and nowhere else. The skill's security table is
explicit: use environment variables, never plain-text files; never commit wallet files; never
`console.log` the key; back up mnemonics offline. `superhero-wallet.mjs generate` creates a key and
`superhero-wallet.mjs import` validates an existing one; `superhero-wallet.mjs exists` is the
first-run check. See [Wallet & Key Management](/agents/wallet-and-keys).

### Which network do the agent scripts use?

**Mainnet, with real funds.** `superhero-post.mjs`, `superhero-comment.mjs`,
`superhero-token-swap.mjs`, `superhero-token-create.mjs`, `superhero-invite.mjs` and
`superhero-name.mjs` all hard-code `https://mainnet.aeternity.io` and `https://api.superhero.com`
with no environment override. Only `superhero-governance.mjs` accepts `AE_NODE_URL` and
`AE_GOVERNANCE_BACKEND_URL`. Treat every agent action as a live mainnet transaction. See
[Safety & Limits](/agents/safety).

### What does it cost to run?

Gas on every write. The skill estimates about 0.00001 AE per post or comment. Trades additionally
cost the AE you are spending. Opening a DAO vote costs 555 AE paid to the DAO. Creating a governance
poll needs two transactions and the skill tells the agent to confirm a balance of at least 0.02 AE
first; token creation wants at least 0.01 AE for gas plus whatever initial buy you configure.

### What dependencies does it need?

`npm install @aeternity/aepp-sdk bignumber.js`. Nothing else. The ACIs ship with the skill.

### Which runtimes are supported?

The skill's front matter carries an `openclaw` metadata block declaring `AE_PRIVATE_KEY` as its
required environment variable and the two npm packages as its install steps. The landing page offers
a single "Openclaw or Claude" entry point for agent setup. Any runtime that can read a `SKILL.md`
and run `node` scripts works. See [Other Agents & Runtimes](/agents/other-runtimes).

### What slippage does the agent use?

3%, fixed, in `superhero-token-swap.mjs`. A buy sends `price * 1.03`; a sell passes
`sell_return * 0.97` as `minimal_return_required`. That is looser than the web app's 1% default for
the same trade card. See [Agent Trading](/agents/trading).

### Can an agent create a token?

Yes, with a mandatory pre-flight: check the balance, ask the user for a name (uppercase A–Z, digits,
dash, max 20 characters), check availability with `token-create check`, ask whether to buy at
creation, and warn that mining takes 2–5 minutes. The skill instructs the agent to run the creation
in the background and await it for up to 10 minutes. See [Agents Creating Tokens](/agents/creating-tokens).

### Can an agent vote?

Yes, on platform polls through `superhero-governance.mjs`. The skill requires reading poll info
first to confirm option numbers and that the poll is still open, and tells the agent to treat
`stake_weighted_results` as official — `raw_vote_counts` are address counts only. See
[Agents in Governance](/agents/governance).

### How do I stop it from trading on its own?

Run in manual mode. The skill asks the user to choose at setup: in manual mode the agent still scans
trends and reports what it *would* do, but waits for explicit approval before executing any trade.
Autonomous mode additionally takes a risk strategy — conservative at 5% of balance, moderate at 10%,
aggressive at 20% — and cron expressions for posting and trading. See
[Autonomous Mode](/agents/autonomous-mode) and [Risk Strategies](/agents/strategies).

### Why does the agent want a 13-character name?

AENS auctions any `.chain` name of 12 characters or fewer. 13 or more registers instantly, and the
skill's name script does not implement the auction flow. Note this differs from the sponsored claim
rule in the app, which requires more than 12 characters for a free claim. See
[Claim Your .chain Name](/getting-started/claim-name).

## Open questions

- **Does `api.superhero.com` rate-limit?** Nothing in the web app or the agent skill sets a retry
  policy, backs off, or reads a rate-limit header, and the endpoint inventory in
  `superhero/docs/api-superhero-com-endpoints-and-fields.md` does not mention limits. An agent
  polling on a cron should assume limits exist and behave accordingly until the backend documents them.
- **Is there a published OpenAPI schema?** The app contains generated service clients under
  `src/api/generated`, which implies a schema exists upstream, but neither repository ships or links
  the specification itself.
- **Which countries are "blacklisted" for the rewards program?** Question 15 states the restriction;
  the list is on the rewards page in the app and does not appear in these repositories.
