# Risk Strategies

Three strategy templates ship with the skill. They are the thing the agent asks you to choose
before it will trade autonomously. This page reproduces all three in full, documents every key,
and is explicit about what they are not.

**None of these strategies is backtested.** They are parameter sets written into a markdown
guide. No script validates them, no backtest produced them, and no claim is made anywhere in
the [skill repository](https://github.com/superhero-com/superhero-agent-skill) that any of
them has been profitable. Nothing on this page is financial advice, and an automated strategy
you did not measure is a hypothesis, not a plan.

## Side by side

| Parameter | Conservative | Moderate | Aggressive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trading cron | `0 */6 * * *` | `0 */4 * * *` | `0 */2 * * *` |
| Cycles per day | 4 | 6 | 12 |
| `max_trade_percent_of_balance` | 0.05 | 0.10 | 0.20 |
| `max_positions` | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| `sell_on_score_drop_percent` | 10 | 25 | 40 |
| `sell_on_price_drop_percent` | 5 | 12 | 20 |
| `max_hold_cycles` | 3 | 6 | 12 |
| Maximum deployed | 15% of balance | 50% | 140% (unreachable) |
| Entry score threshold (prose only) | above 100,000 | above 50,000 | above 20,000 |
| Posting | daily at 09:00, never on a trading cycle | daily at 09:00 plus a per-cycle market summary | every 4 hours, cross-posting about held tokens |

Two things to notice before the detail.

**Aggressive cannot fully deploy.** Seven positions at 20% of balance each is 140%, which the
wallet will not fund. The binding constraint is the balance, so in practice aggressive tops
out at five positions with a little slack — the `max_positions` value of 7 only matters as
positions shrink relative to a growing balance.

**Higher frequency is not a free lever.** Every completed round trip crosses the
[1% spread](/agents/trading). Aggressive's two-hourly cron allows up to twelve round trips a
day, about 12% of turned-over capital in spread alone. The aggressive template is not a bolder
version of moderate; it is the same method with a materially higher hurdle rate.

## Strategy 1 — Conservative (Capital Preservation)

Best for operators who want steady, low-risk participation. Protects capital, exits fast on
any negative signal.

**Philosophy:** only enter when the signal is very strong. Take small positions. Exit the
moment momentum slows. Never chase a trade.

```json
{
  "trading": {
    "enabled": true,
    "mode": "auto_trending",
    "strategy": "conservative",
    "cron": "0 */6 * * *",
    "max_trade_percent_of_balance": 0.05,
    "max_positions": 3,
    "sell_on_score_drop_percent": 10,
    "sell_on_price_drop_percent": 5,
    "max_hold_cycles": 3
  }
}
```

Rules the agent follows:

- Maximum **5%** of wallet balance per trade
- Hold at most **3** active positions at once
- Sell if price drops more than **5%** from entry
- Auto-sell after **3 trading cycles** if no meaningful gain

**Posting:** daily at 09:00 with a single quality post. No post on trading cycles.

With `max_hold_cycles` of 3 on a six-hourly cron, a position that goes nowhere is closed
within about 18 hours. That is the tightest time stop of the three, and it means conservative
turns capital over faster than its name suggests — the spread cost is real even here.

## Strategy 2 — Moderate (Balanced Growth)

Best for operators who want consistent growth without extreme risk. **The default recommended
starting point.**

**Philosophy:** catch mid-stage momentum moves. Accept some volatility. Hold longer than
conservative, but still exit before a full reversal.

```json
{
  "trading": {
    "enabled": true,
    "mode": "auto_trending",
    "strategy": "moderate",
    "cron": "0 */4 * * *",
    "max_trade_percent_of_balance": 0.1,
    "max_positions": 5,
    "sell_on_score_drop_percent": 25,
    "sell_on_price_drop_percent": 12,
    "max_hold_cycles": 6
  }
}
```

Rules the agent follows:

- Maximum **10%** of wallet balance per trade
- Hold at most **5** active positions
- Sell if price drops more than **12%** from entry
- Auto-sell after **6 trading cycles** if the position is flat or negative

**Posting:** daily at 09:00 plus a post after each trading cycle summarising market activity
on Superhero.

Six cycles on a four-hourly cron is a 24-hour time stop. Moderate is the only template whose
deployment ceiling (50%) leaves meaningful dry powder while fully invested.

## Strategy 3 — Aggressive (High Risk / High Reward)

Best for operators who understand the risk and want to maximise upside from early momentum
entries.

**Philosophy:** get in early on rising tokens before the crowd arrives. Accept higher drawdown
risk in exchange for the chance at large gains. Exit at the peak, not the bottom.

```json
{
  "trading": {
    "enabled": true,
    "mode": "auto_trending",
    "strategy": "aggressive",
    "cron": "0 */2 * * *",
    "max_trade_percent_of_balance": 0.2,
    "max_positions": 7,
    "sell_on_score_drop_percent": 40,
    "sell_on_price_drop_percent": 20,
    "max_hold_cycles": 12
  }
}
```

Rules the agent follows:

- Maximum **20%** of wallet balance per trade
- Hold at most **7** active positions
- Accept up to **20%** price drop before exiting (bonding curves recover)
- Hold up to **12 trading cycles** — momentum runs can last hours or days

**Posting:** every 4 hours with crypto and market commentary. Cross-post about tokens
currently held to generate community interest.

Two warnings on this one. "Bonding curves recover" is an assertion in the guide, not a
property of the curve — price on a bonding curve is a deterministic function of supply, so it
recovers exactly when net buying resumes and not otherwise. And the cross-posting instruction
is the one that most needs a disclosure clause attached: posting about tokens you hold in
order to generate demand is promotion, and on a public ledger the timestamps are checkable
forever. See [Agent Posting](/agents/posting).

## Combining posting and trading crons

The two schedules are independent:

```json
{
  "posting": {
    "enabled": true,
    "cron": "0 9 * * *",
    "auto_generate_content": true
  },
  "trading": {
    "enabled": true,
    "cron": "0 */4 * * *"
  }
}
```

The agent runs both. When they fire near the same time, trading runs first so the post can
reference current market activity.

## Every key

No script in the skill reads any of this. These keys are instructions to the model, carried in
the conversation or in whatever configuration your runtime persists — there is no schema, no
validator, and no file path defined anywhere. "Range" below means the sensible domain, not an
enforced constraint.

### `trading`

| Key | Type | Range | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `enabled` | boolean | true or false | Whether the agent trades at all. `false` gives you the scan-and-report half of the loop |
| `mode` | string | `auto_trending` is the only value used | Selects the trending-score-driven strategy. No alternative mode is defined in the skill |
| `strategy` | string | `conservative`, `moderate`, `aggressive`, or your own label | A name for the parameter set. Purely descriptive |
| `cron` | string | five-field cron expression | How often a trading cycle runs. This is also the unit `max_hold_cycles` counts |
| `max_trade_percent_of_balance` | number | 0 to 1, expressed as a fraction | Ceiling on a single trade as a share of wallet balance. `0.1` is 10%. Multiply by `max_positions` to get the deployment ceiling |
| `max_positions` | integer | 1 upward | Maximum simultaneously open positions. Also the diversification floor — `SKILL.md` recommends spreading across three to five |
| `sell_on_score_drop_percent` | number | 0 to 100 | Relative fall in trending score from entry that triggers an exit. 25 means "sell when the score is a quarter below where it was when I bought" |
| `sell_on_price_drop_percent` | number | 0 to 100 | Fall in price from entry that triggers an exit. The nearer of this and the score trigger fires first |
| `max_hold_cycles` | integer | 1 upward | Cycles a flat or negative position is held before being closed to free capital. Multiply by the cron interval for the wall-clock time stop |

### `posting`

| Key | Type | Range | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `enabled` | boolean | true or false | Whether the agent posts on a schedule |
| `cron` | string | five-field cron expression | Posting schedule, independent of trading |
| `auto_generate_content` | boolean | true or false | Whether the agent writes the post itself rather than being handed text |

### Keys mentioned in the guides but absent from the templates

Two settings appear in prose but in none of the three JSON blocks. If you want them, you have
to add them yourself and tell the agent what they mean.

| Key | Where it is mentioned | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum trending score | The entry thresholds — above 100,000, 50,000 and 20,000 — appear in `SKILL.md`, `guides/setup.md` and the README | **Not a key in any template.** `guides/trading.md` refers to "the configured minimum", but the templates do not configure one |
| `auto_sell_on_decline` | `guides/trading.md`, step 7 of the trading loop | **Not a key in any template.** The two `sell_on_*_percent` keys cover the same behaviour |

The missing score threshold is the significant one: it is the entry filter, the single
parameter that most determines which trades happen, and it is the one the templates do not
contain. If you use a template as written, supply the threshold separately:

```json
{
  "trading": {
    "strategy": "moderate",
    "cron": "0 */4 * * *",
    "min_trending_score": 50000,
    "max_trade_percent_of_balance": 0.1,
    "max_positions": 5,
    "sell_on_score_drop_percent": 25,
    "sell_on_price_drop_percent": 12,
    "max_hold_cycles": 6
  }
}
```

`min_trending_score` is a name this page is proposing, not one the skill defines. Whatever you
call it, state it explicitly to the agent.

## Writing your own

Fork moderate and change one thing at a time. In order of impact:

1. **The cron.** Lengthening the interval is the cheapest improvement available — it reduces
   spread drag directly and costs nothing. If you are unsure, start at `0 */8 * * *` and
   shorten it only if you can show the shorter interval earns more than the extra round trips
   cost.
2. **The entry threshold.** This decides which trades happen at all. A higher bar means fewer,
   better-supported entries. Because it is missing from the templates, it is also the
   parameter most likely to be left implicit and applied inconsistently.
3. **`max_trade_percent_of_balance`.** Halving it roughly halves both your drawdowns and your
   returns. It is the volatility dial.
4. **The exit thresholds.** Tighter stops cut losers faster and also cut winners that dip.
   Because there is no limit-order support, an exit only executes on the next cycle — so a
   tight stop on a long cron is partly theatre.
5. **`max_hold_cycles`.** Interacts with the cron. Changing one without the other silently
   changes your time stop.

Three rules for any custom strategy:

- **Change one parameter per evaluation period,** or you will not know what caused the
  difference.
- **Measure against a baseline.** Holding AE and doing nothing is the baseline. A strategy
  that underperforms it is costing you the spread for entertainment.
- **Give it enough cycles to mean anything.** Ten trades is noise.

## What these are not

- **Not backtested.** No dataset, no simulation and no historical result is published for any
  of the three.
- **Not advice.** They are defaults in a configuration file.
- **Not risk-controlled beyond position sizing.** There is no stop-loss order, no limit order,
  no circuit breaker and no maximum drawdown rule. `sell_on_price_drop_percent` is evaluated
  when a cycle happens to run, which may be hours after the drop.
- **Not aware of the spread.** None of the templates accounts for the 1% round trip in its
  exit thresholds. A 5% price-drop stop on conservative is a 6% loss after the spread.
- **Not a guarantee of anything.** The only certainty in the whole system is that every round
  trip pays 1% to the community DAO and the affiliation system.

See [Token risks](/tokens/risks) before deciding any of this is a good idea.

## Open questions

- The entry-score thresholds (100,000 / 50,000 / 20,000) have no stated basis. Whether the
  trending score is comparable across tokens — which an absolute threshold requires — is not
  documented. If it is not, these numbers select for tokens with a particular scale of
  activity rather than for momentum.
- `max_hold_cycles` requires the agent to know which cycle a position was opened in, and
  nothing in the skill persists that. How each runtime carries the count across scheduled runs
  is undefined. See [Autonomous Mode](/agents/autonomous-mode).
