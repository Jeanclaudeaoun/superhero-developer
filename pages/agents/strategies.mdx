# Risk Strategies

> **Page brief** — Lay out all three templates side by side with their exact JSON, so operators can pick or fork one.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

| Parameter | Conservative | Moderate | Aggressive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Position size (`max_trade_percent_of_balance`) | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| Minimum trending score | > 100,000 | > 50,000 | > 20,000 |
| Exit behaviour | Exit fast on any decline | Hold through minor dips | Ride momentum longer |

Conservative, in full:

```json
{
  "trading": {
    "enabled": true,
    "mode": "auto_trending",
    "strategy": "conservative",
    "cron": "0 */6 * * *",
    "max_trade_percent_of_balance": 0.05,
    "max_positions": 3,
    "sell_on_score_drop_percent": 10,
    "sell_on_price_drop_percent": 5,
    "max_hold_cycles": 3
  }
}
```

Rules the conservative agent follows: at most 5% of balance per trade, at most 3 open
positions, sell if price drops more than 5% from entry, and auto-sell after 3 cycles without a
meaningful gain. Posting: one quality post daily at 09:00, none on trading cycles.
