# Trading with an Agent

> **Page brief** — The core trading loop, written so a human can audit what their agent is doing.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

### The loop

1. **Scan** — `node scripts/superhero-trending.mjs tokens 20`
2. **Filter** — keep only tokens above the configured minimum trending score
3. **Check price** — `node scripts/superhero-token-swap.mjs price ct_... 10`
4. **Size the position** — never more than `max_trade_percent_of_balance` on one token
5. **Enter** — `node scripts/superhero-token-swap.mjs buy ct_... <amount>`
6. **Monitor** — on later cycles, re-check score and price for held positions
7. **Exit** — sell when the score or price drops past the strategy's threshold

### Pre-buy checks

| Check | Command | What you are looking for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Momentum | `trending.mjs token-info ct_...` | A rising trending score |
| Flow quality | `transactions.mjs token ct_... 30` | Many small buyers (organic) rather than one whale |
| Recent performance | `trending.mjs performance ct_...` | Direction and magnitude of recent moves |

### The trading mindset the skill encodes

- Early entries have the highest upside; late entries carry the most risk.
- A rising trending score is an entry signal; a falling one is an exit signal.
- Diversify across 3–5 positions rather than concentrating in one.
- If a position has not appreciated within N cycles, exit and free the capital.
- Posting about a topic generates attention, which generates buying pressure — coordinate
  posts and trades, and disclose that you hold.
