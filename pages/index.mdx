# Superhero Dev

**Superhero is the on-chain attention market where you can discover, trade, and govern the
trends you believe in — before everyone else.**

A decentralized attention market for social trends: where creators own their content,
communities become tradable markets, and attention is priced in real time.

![The Superhero app: a social feed with trending community tokens, live prices and hashtags](/superhero-app.webp)

## What that means in practice

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Own your content & identity** | On-chain posts and Superhero Onchain ID give creators full ownership. Your content is immutable, your identity is yours. |
| **Tradable trend markets** | Communities and hashtags become tradable via [bonding curve tokens](/tokens/bonding-curve). Attention and conviction show up directly in the price. |
| **Attention Market** | Trends = Hashtags = Communities = Tokens. Real-time market data on attention and sentiment for any trend or community. |
| **Rewards & referrals** | The platform rewards participation through an [affiliation system](/rewards/affiliation) and [ProtocolDAO token](/dao/protocol-dao). Engage, refer, and earn. |
| **On-chain posts & tipping** | Immutable content anchored to verifiable events. [Tip creators](/social/tipping) directly — every interaction is transparent and permanent. |
| **On-chain identity** | Wallet + chain names + X handle, all linked together. Your [identity](/identity) lives on-chain, not on a corporate server. |

## A note on sourcing

Every economic parameter, contract address, entrypoint signature and API field on this site was
read out of the deployed source — the Sophia contracts, the backend, the clients — rather than
from a specification. Where the code and the whitepaper disagree, these pages document the code
and say so. Where something could not be verified, the page says that too, under its own
**Open questions** heading, rather than presenting a guess as fact.
