# Superhero Documentation

> **Page brief** — The front door. It must say what Superhero is in three sentences, then route the reader to the right track (user, builder, or AI agent).
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

**Superhero is an on-chain attention market.** Posts, identities, communities and the
tokens that price them all live on a public blockchain, so content cannot be deleted,
identity cannot be revoked, and every fee split is verifiable by anyone.

Three things make it different from a normal social app:

1. **You own your content and your identity.** Posts are written to a smart contract, not
   a company database. Your identity is a wallet address plus the names you attach to it.
2. **Trends are tradable.** Every hashtag, topic or community can become a token priced by
   an [exponential bonding curve](/tokens/bonding-curve). Attention shows up directly in the price.
3. **Communities govern their own money.** Each token has a DAO treasury funded by trading
   fees, and its holders vote on how it is spent.
