# Fees & Slippage

Three different things move the number. The **curve spread** is a fixed 1% of the curve value,
split between the community DAO and the affiliation system. **Curve slippage** is the cost of your
own trade size, and depends on how big it is relative to the supply. **Gas** is the æternity network
fee, and the smallest of the three by a wide margin.

## 1. The spread

The spread is not a line item deducted from your trade. It is the gap between two curves:

```text
buy(x)  = 0.001 · e^(0.00000001 · x) − 0.0009999
sell(x) = buy(x) · 0.995
```

The sale contract mints against `buy` and redeems against `sell`. The 0.5% difference accrues to the
community's DAO treasury. On top of that, the sale contract charges a **0.5% affiliation fee on
buys**, so the total spread between buy and sell price is **1%**.

### `price()` already includes the affiliation fee

On the sale contract:

```sophia
entrypoint price(count : int) : int =
  require(count > 0, "COUNT_NOT_POSITIVE")
  let bonding_curve_price = state.bonding_curve.calculate_buy_price(state.token_contract.total_supply(), count)
  let affiliation_amount = affiliation_amount(bonding_curve_price)
  bonding_curve_price + affiliation_amount

function affiliation_amount(bonding_curve_price : int) =
  let affiliation_percentage = state.affiliation_treasury.get_affiliation_fee_percentage()
  (bonding_curve_price * affiliation_percentage) / state.affiliation_treasury.get_affiliation_fee_precision()
```

| Call | What it returns |
| --- | --- |
| `BondingCurve.calculate_buy_price(supply, count)` | The bare curve integral |
| `TokenSale.price(count)` | The curve integral **plus** the 0.5% affiliation fee |
| `TokenSale.sell_return(count)` | `BondingCurve.calculate_sell_return(supply, count)` verbatim — no fee added or removed |

**Attach exactly `price(count)` and you are fully covered.** Do not multiply it by 1.005 again. The
web app applies the 1.005 to the *curve* figure it computes off-chain, not to `price()`; the CLI
sends `price()` plus a slippage buffer and nothing else. Neither double-charges.

The fee percentage is read live from the affiliation treasury on every call:
`AffiliationTreasury.get_affiliation_fee_percentage()` returns `500` and
`get_affiliation_fee_precision()` returns `10^5`, so the fee is `500 / 100000 = 0.5%`. `500` is the
sum of the per-level percentages `[300, 125, 50, 25]`.

### Surplus AE is refunded

`buy` requires `Call.value >= total_price` rather than equality, then returns the difference:

```sophia
require(Call.value >= total_price, "AE_AMOUNT_NOT_SUFFICIENT")
...
let overpaid_refund = Call.value - total_price
if (overpaid_refund > 0) Chain.spend(Call.caller, overpaid_refund) else ()
```

`CommunityFactory.create_community` does the same before it forwards the initial buy. A slippage
buffer costs you nothing when the price does not move — you get the unused portion back in the same
transaction. The only cost of a generous buffer is that the AE has to be in your wallet when you sign.

### Where each AE goes

```text
Buyer pays AE
  ├── 99.0%  → Bonding Curve Reserve (redeemable by sellers)
  ├──  0.5%  → Community DAO Treasury (governed by token holders)
  └──  0.5%  → Affiliation System
                ├── 0.300% → Level 1 Referrer
                ├── 0.125% → Level 2 Referrer
                ├── 0.050% → Level 3 Referrer
                └── 0.025% → Level 4 Referrer
                (if no referrer: burned)

Buyer also receives:
  └── ProtocolDAO Tokens = 1,000 × AE value
```

The four-level split is credited per level as the treasury walks up the affiliation tree.
`AffiliationTreasury.record_sale_transaction(buyer, transaction_value)` credits `300`, then `125`,
then `50`, then `25` hundred-thousandths of the transaction value to successive inviters, and stops
as soon as an account has no inviter recorded. Levels with no inviter are not paid out; that share
is burned.

Credited rewards are withdrawable for the current and the previous 30-day payout period
(`payout_period_interval = 2592000000` milliseconds) and expire after that. `withdraw()` also
requires the caller to have reached four unique invitees who each bought at least 10 AE
(`unique_invitee_count_threshold = 4`, `unique_invitee_buy_amount_threshold = 10 * 10^18`);
below that bar it aborts with `MINIMUM_ACCOUNTS_THRESHOLD_NOT_REACHED`. See
[Affiliation rewards](/rewards/affiliation).

The live percentages are readable on-chain: `fee_percentage()` and `fee_precision()` on the
factory, and `get_affiliation_fee_percentage()`, `get_affiliation_fee_precision()` and
`get_affiliation_fee_percentages()` on the affiliation treasury.

### The DAO's half, exactly

The 0.5% that reaches the community DAO is the residue after the reserve is set aside, in
`buy_with_affiliation_inner`:

```sophia
let reserve = state.bonding_curve.calculate_sell_return(state.token_contract.total_supply(), count)
state.dao_contract.record_sale_transaction(
  value = Call.value - overpaid_refund - affiliation_amount - reserve, protected = true,
  Call.caller, total_price)
```

`total_supply()` has already been incremented by the mint, so `reserve` is exactly
`0.995 × bonding_curve_price` — the AE that would be owed if the buyer immediately sold back. The
DAO gets `bonding_curve_price − reserve`, the remaining `0.5%`, and the sale contract keeps the
reserve. The payout obligation is computed first and the treasury receives only what is left over,
which is why the reserve is always solvent.

Both external calls use `protected = true`, so a failing DAO or treasury call cannot block a trade —
the value stays in the sale contract instead of reverting the buy.

### What 1% costs you in practice

Buy 1,000 tokens at a supply of 10,000,000 and immediately sell them back:

```text
curve integral for the buy         0.105276443949 AE
× 1.005 affiliation fee            0.105802826168 AE   ← what you pay

curve integral for the sell        0.105276443949 AE
× 0.995 sell factor                0.104750061729 AE   ← what you get back

difference                         0.001052764439 AE
```

That is **1% of the curve value, and 0.995% of the AE you handed over**; the two differ because the
fee is added on top of the curve price on the way in. Every position starts about 1% down — see
[Risks](/tokens/risks).

The spread is charged on value, not on trade count, so ten small round trips cost the same 1% each
as one large one. There is no fixed component to amortise.

## 2. Curve slippage

Slippage on a bonding curve is arithmetic, not congestion. You pay the integral of a rising curve
over the range you mint into, while the quoted spot price is only the value at the left edge of that
range. The larger your trade relative to the supply, the further above spot your average fill lands.

Buying from a supply of 10,000,000, where spot is `1.052709e-4 AE`:

| Tokens bought | Share of existing supply | Total cost (AE) | Average price (AE) | Premium to spot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 | 0.01% | 0.105276 | 1.05276e-4 | +0.005% |
| 100,000 | 1% | 10.582369 | 1.05824e-4 | +0.53% |
| 1,000,000 | 10% | 110.815238 | 1.10815e-4 | +5.27% |
| 10,000,000 | 100% | 1,624.184008 | 1.62418e-4 | +54.3% |
| 50,000,000 | 500% | 21,699.788231 | 4.33996e-4 | +312% |

Rule of thumb: **your premium to spot is roughly half the percentage of the supply you are adding**,
up to about 10%, and worse than that beyond it.

Splitting the order does not help. The curve is path-independent — ten buys of 1,000,000 from a
rising supply cost exactly the same total as one buy of 10,000,000, because the integral is the same
area either way. The only levers are trade size and the supply you trade at.

Near supply zero the effect is far larger in relative terms, because the `−0.0009999` offset has
cancelled almost all of the exponential: buying the first 1,000 tokens of a brand-new token costs
5% above spot. See [Bonding Curves](/tokens/bonding-curve).

## 3. Slippage guards

A guard is not a fee. It is the tolerance you accept for what other people do between your quote and
your fill.

| Surface | Default | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web app trade card | **1%** | Stored per browser under the local-storage key `tokenTrade:slippage`; adjustable in the trade card |
| `superhero-token-swap.mjs buy` | **3%** | Attaches `price(count) × 1.03` as the transaction amount |
| `superhero-token-swap.mjs sell` | **3%** | Passes `sell_return(count) × 0.97` as `minimal_return_required` |
| `superhero-token-create.mjs create` | **5%** | Attaches `calculate_buy_price(0, count) × 1.05`, rounded up |

To change the CLI tolerance, edit `SLIPPAGE_PERCENT` at the top of the swap script. To set your own
precisely, call the contract directly, as shown in [Buying & Selling](/tokens/buying-and-selling).

The guards are asymmetric:

- **On a buy**, the guard is an upper bound on spend. You attach more AE than the quote; anything the trade does not need is refunded in the same transaction, and if the price moved further than your tolerance the call reverts with `AE_AMOUNT_NOT_SUFFICIENT`.
- **On a sell**, the guard is `minimal_return_required` — a floor on the payout. Below it the call reverts with `MINIMAL_RETURN_NOT_MEET` and your tokens stay where they are.

Because surplus is refunded, a buy buffer is close to free and a sell floor is a real decision. Size
the sell floor carefully; be generous with the buy buffer.

## 4. Gas

Every transaction pays an æternity network fee in AE, independent of everything above.

| Action | Transactions | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create a token | 1 contract call | About 0.001 AE |
| Buy | 1 contract call | Depends on contract execution |
| Sell | 2 contract calls — allowance, then sell | Depends on contract execution |
| Post | 1 contract call | Depends on contract execution |

Read the exact fee off any mined transaction in [aeScan](https://aescan.io). Two things hold
regardless: a **sell costs two transactions**, not one, because of the allowance step; and the web
app never lets you spend your whole balance. Its "spendable" figure is
`balance − balance × (slippage + 1) / 100 − 0.001 AE`, so at the default 1% tolerance it holds back
2% of your balance plus a flat 0.001 AE for the fee.

For what the gas is paying for, see [AE and gas](/aeternity/ae-and-gas).

## Putting it together

For a buy of `n` tokens at supply `S`:

```text
AE leaving your wallet
  = ∫ from S to S+n of buy(x) dx     ← the curve, including your own slippage
  × 1.005                             ← the affiliation fee; together, this is price(n)
  + network gas
  ( any buffer you attached above price(n) is refunded in the same transaction )
```

And for the matching sell later, at whatever supply `S'` prevails then:

```text
AE returning to your wallet
  = ∫ from S'−n to S' of buy(x) dx
  × 0.995                             ← the sell factor, i.e. the DAO's cut
  − network gas
  ( reverts entirely if below minimal_return_required )
```

Nothing else is charged. There is no listing fee, no withdrawal fee, no maker or taker fee, and no
fee for holding.
