# Risks

> **Page brief** — An honest risk page. It protects users and it is the page a regulator or a journalist will read first.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

Volatility and smart-contract risk are real. Double-check addresses and amounts before
signing. Never risk funds you cannot afford to lose.
