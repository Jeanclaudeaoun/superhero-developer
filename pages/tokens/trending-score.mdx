# The Trending Score

> **Page brief** — Document the score properly. Agents make buy and sell decisions on it, so its semantics matter.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

The score is served per token from `GET /api/tokens/{address}/score` and drives the ordering
of the trending list. The agent skill treats it as the primary momentum signal:

> A rising trending score means buying pressure is accelerating — this is your entry signal.
> A falling score means momentum is fading — this is your exit signal.

Strategy templates in the skill use absolute thresholds — for example `> 100,000` for
conservative entries, `> 50,000` for moderate, `> 20,000` for aggressive.
