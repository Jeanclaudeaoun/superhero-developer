# Bonding Curves

> **Page brief** — The flagship explainer. Define what a bonding curve is, give the exact Superhero formulas, and show why the reserve is always solvent.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

A bonding curve is a function that maps circulating supply to price. Buying mints new tokens
and moves you up the curve; selling burns tokens and moves you down it. Because price is
computed from supply, there is always a quote and always liquidity — no order book, no
matching, no counterparty.

Superhero uses an **exponential** curve, specified in `BondingCurveExponential.aes` and
implementing the interface in `BondingCurve.aes`.

### The formulas

```text
buy(x)  = 0.001 · e^(0.00000001 · x) − 0.0009999

sell(x) = buy(x) · 0.995
```

The price you pay to buy `amount` tokens at a given `supply` is the integral of the buy curve
over the range you are minting into:

```text
buy price   = ∫ from supply to (supply + amount) of buy(x) dx

sell return = ∫ from (supply − amount) to supply of sell(x) dx
```

And the spread — the community DAO's benefit — is the area between the two curves:

```text
spread = ∫ from 0 to supply of buy(x) dx  −  ∫ from 0 to supply of sell(x) dx
```

### Why the reserve is always solvent

The sell curve sits strictly below the buy curve at every supply level. That guarantees the
reserve held in the token sale contract is always sufficient to buy back the **entire**
circulating supply, so a bank-run scenario is structurally impossible. Selling the whole
supply back to the curve is always possible.

### The spread

The `sell()` curve returns 99.5% of `buy()`, and the affiliation token sale contract adds a
further 0.5% affiliation fee — so the **total spread between buy and sell price is 1%**:
0.5% to the community DAO and 0.5% to the affiliation system.

### Design intent

The curve is tuned on the expectation that only **10–20% of the total supply** will ever be
reached, which puts a noticeable price increase right at the start of the curve.

### Reading the curve from the contract

| Entrypoint | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `buy_price(total_supply)` | Instantaneous buy price at a supply level |
| `sell_price(total_supply)` | Instantaneous sell price at a supply level |
| `calculate_buy_price(total_supply, count)` | Cost to buy `count` tokens from `total_supply` |
| `calculate_sell_return(total_supply, sell_tokens)` | AE returned for selling `sell_tokens` |
| `buy_curve(x)` / `sell_curve(x)` | The curve value as a `Frac.frac` |
| `get_k()`, `get_initial_price()`, `get_minimum_count()` | Curve parameters |
| `get_sell_return_percentage()`, `get_sell_return_precision()` | The 99.5% sell factor and its precision |
| `curve_type()`, `supported_decimals()` | Curve identity and decimal handling |
