# Reading a Token Page

> **Page brief** — Teach a reader to evaluate a token from its page rather than from its price alone.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

Price and market cap are displayed in AE. Trending defaults to Market Cap so larger
communities surface first. Token pages include candlestick charts, recent transactions and
holders.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Holders | Top addresses and their balances |
| Transactions | Recent buys and sells with timestamps and transaction hashes |
