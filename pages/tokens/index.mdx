# Tokens & Bonding Curves

> **Page brief** — The most important section on the site. It must leave a reader able to launch a token, price a trade by hand, and explain the fee split from memory.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

Every Superhero community token is an AEX-9 token minted and burned against an **exponential
bonding curve**. There is no order book and no counterparty: you always trade against the
curve, price is a pure function of supply, and the contract always holds enough reserve to buy
the entire supply back.

Start with [Bonding Curves](/tokens/bonding-curve) — the rest of this section assumes it.
