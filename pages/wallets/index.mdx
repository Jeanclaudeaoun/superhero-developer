# Wallets

> **Page brief** — The complete wallet section — installation on every surface, connection flows, backup, recovery and security. This is the section most often linked from support.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

Superhero works with two different things people call "a wallet":

1. **Superhero Wallet** — the standalone æternity wallet. Browser extension, native mobile
   apps, and a web wallet at `wallet.superhero.com`.
2. **The built-in wallet** — a wallet created and held inside the Superhero app itself,
   unlocked with a passkey or passphrase. This is what the mobile app means by
   "wallet built in, no extensions, no friction".

Both hold the same kind of æternity account (`ak_...`). Pick based on how you use the app.
