# How DAOs Work

> **Page brief** — The DAO orientation page — the lifecycle from funding to execution.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

1. Trading fees accumulate in the DAO treasury (0.5% of every buy).
2. Any holder above the community threshold can open a vote, paying **555 AE** to the DAO.
3. The vote stays open for **two weeks**, measured in key-blocks.
4. It passes with at least **55% of the vote stake in favour** and at least **10% of total
   token stake participating**.
5. A passed vote must be applied within roughly **two days** of closing. Any account — or a
   script — can trigger execution.
