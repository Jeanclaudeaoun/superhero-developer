# Quorum & Thresholds

> **Page brief** — A short, precise reference page. People will link to it to settle arguments.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: reference`

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Cost to open a vote | 555 AE, paid to the DAO |
| Voting period | 2 weeks, measured in key-blocks |
| Approval threshold | At least 55% of the vote stake in favour |
| Participation (quorum) | At least 10% of total token stake |
| Execution window | Approximately 2 days after the vote closes |
| Who can execute | Any account, or a script, via the DAO contract |
| Who can open a vote | Holders at or above the community threshold amount |
