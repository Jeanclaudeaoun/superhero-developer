# Voting

> **Page brief** — Document the voting mechanics including the allowance and locking behaviour, which surprises people.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: deep`

| Action | Entrypoint | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vote | `vote(option, amount)` | Records for or against. The tokens must be **allowed** for the vote contract and are **locked** by it. |
| Revoke | `revoke_vote()` | Retract your vote and reclaim your locked stake. |
| Withdraw | `withdraw()` | After the closing height, reclaim your stake without affecting the outcome. |

Read-only helpers: `current_vote_state()`, `final_vote_state()`, `has_voted(voter)`,
`voted_option(voter)`, `has_withdrawn(voter)`, `is_closed()`, `close_height()`.

Tokens locked in a vote cannot be transferred or sold until you revoke or withdraw — this is
what prevents vote manipulation by moving tokens between addresses during an active vote.
