# The DAO Treasury

> **Page brief** — Document treasury funding, balance visibility, and the `record_sale_transaction` path.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: standard`

The treasury is funded by 0.5% of every buy on the community's bonding curve, plus the 555 AE
deposit from every vote that is opened.

Proceeds reach the DAO through `record_sale_transaction(buyer, buy_price)` rather than a bare
`Chain.spend`, which is what makes upgraded DAOs able to add their own logic for splitting or
using proceeds.
