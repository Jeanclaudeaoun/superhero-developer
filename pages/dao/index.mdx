# Community DAOs

> **Page brief** — Document the DAO layer completely — treasury, proposal types, voting rules, execution, and migration.
>
> `status: outline` · `depth: overview`

Every community token has a DAO. The `DAO.aes` contract is both the **owner** and the
**beneficiary** of the token sale: it receives the trading proceeds and holds administrative
authority over the sale. Token holders decide what happens to that money.
