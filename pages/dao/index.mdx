# Community DAOs

Every community token created through the Superhero factory gets its own DAO. The `DAO.aes`
contract is both the **owner** and the **beneficiary** of that token's sale: it receives the
trading proceeds and holds administrative authority over the sale contract. Token holders
decide what happens to that money by opening proposals and voting with their tokens.

This section documents that system end to end — where the money comes from, what can be
proposed, how voting and token locking work, the exact thresholds a proposal must clear, and
how a community can replace its DAO with a different implementation.

## Community DAOs are not æternity governance polls

These are two separate systems with separate contracts, separate voting power and separate
user interfaces. Readers confuse them constantly.

| | Community DAO | æternity governance poll |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contracts | `DAO.aes`, `DAOVote.aes` | `Registry.aes`, `Poll.aes` |
| Scope | One community token's treasury | The æternity ecosystem as a whole |
| Voting power | That community's AEX-9 token balance | AE balance, plus delegated AE |
| Tokens locked while voting | Yes — an AEX-9 allowance locks your stake | No |
| Ballot shape | Binary: in favour or against | Any list of at least two options |
| Outcome | Executes an on-chain action | Signalling; no automatic execution |
| Where in the app | `/trends/daos` and `/trends/dao/:saleAddress` | `/voting` |
| Documented in | This section | [Governance and Voting](/governance) |

If you are looking for stake-weighted ecosystem polls, delegation, or the `/voting` tab,
go to [Governance and Voting](/governance) instead.

## The shape of a community DAO

A community's contracts are deployed together, in one transaction, by
`CommunityFactory.create_community`. Four of them matter here:

| Contract | Role in governance |
| --- | --- |
| `FungibleTokenFull` (AEX-9) | The community token. Balances are voting power. |
| `AffiliationBondingCurveTokenSale` | Mints and burns tokens; sends the DAO its fee share. |
| `DAO` | Holds the treasury, registers votes, executes passed proposals. |
| `CommunityManagement` | Moderators, mute list, write threshold, community metadata. |

`DAO` is the owner and beneficiary of the token sale, and it can reach
`CommunityManagement` through `get_community_management()`. That is why a single vote can
both pay out treasury AE and appoint a moderator — the DAO has authority over both.

## The lifecycle in one pass

1. Trading fees accumulate in the DAO treasury — 0.5% of every buy.
2. A holder at or above the community threshold opens a vote, paying **555 AE** to the DAO.
3. The vote stays open for **two weeks**, measured in key-blocks.
4. Holders vote for or against by locking tokens in the vote contract.
5. It passes with at least **55% of the voted stake in favour** and at least **10% of total
   token stake participating**.
6. A passed vote must be applied within roughly **two days** of closing. Any account can
   trigger execution.
7. Voters withdraw their locked tokens.
